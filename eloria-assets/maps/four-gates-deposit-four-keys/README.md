# The Deposit of Four Keys

`four-gates-deposit-four-keys` — an interior of Four Gates, in the civic quarter.

Storage and banking; four keyed vault doors and a plastered fifth.

## Provenance

Every mesh and every texture in this package is generated by
`eloria-assets/tools/four_gates/build_interiors.py` from the primitives in
`meshlib.py`, the interior kit in `interiors.py` and the procedural material
synthesis in `texturing.py`. Nothing is imported, traced, converted or
extracted from any other game; no third-party asset pack is used. Rebuild it
with:

    python3 eloria-assets/tools/four_gates/build_interiors.py

## Entering and leaving

The door on the street is placed from `interior_index.py`, which is the single
source of truth shared by the city build and this package, so the shopfront and
the exit portal cannot drift apart. Entering and leaving is an ordinary
`CHANGE_MAP`; the server remains authoritative over the player's position and
no protocol change was needed.

## Camera

Interiors are closed boxes, so this manifest carries its own `camera` profile
and a `cutaway` block naming the roof and the four walls. The client hides the
roof and whichever wall stands between the camera and the room. Collision is
unaffected -- a cut-away wall is still solid.
