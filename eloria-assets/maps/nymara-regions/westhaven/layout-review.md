# Westhaven compact landscape review

The concept remains a working harbour with terracotta roofs above a curved mole and two bare island silhouettes. The compact layout replaces the old uniform scaling assumption with explicit anchors, road stations, house plots and service space in `source/landscape_plan.py` and `source/layout.py`.

The public quay stays at 3.4m. It has a quiet through-strip, cargo clusters near loading doors, two distinct working piers and a shipyard slip. Warehouse fronts face the water; their doors connect to the quay or lower street. The market and cart routes offer separate climbs instead of sending all traffic through one stair. The civic court has a coherent retaining edge and graded entry gap; three full-sized house plots were moved off the higher civic foundations. The civic skyline retains the Haven Church, Long Arcade, Bell Tower, Astronomers’ Hall and Watch Spire.

The land is prepared first: a continuous city hillside, a lower pasture shoulder and reduced island ridges precede individual foundations. Final grades protect road joints from field preparation. Houses stay full-sized, with purposeful gaps for paths, benches, sacks and small fences. A low shingle wrack shelf provides dry footing and a broad habitat behind the coastal path; it preserves the existing shore wildlife and resource counts under strict placement. Exposed shore vegetation is limited; cultivated ground stays in the sheltered upland.

Gullstone is a rocky island reached on a 4.6m footbridge with water beneath. The Lamp route follows the tidal neck on a 5.2m causeway. Neither route adds a new transport service. Ferry berths retain their destinations and gameplay roles. The lighthouse entry stands outside the upper gallery’s vertical projection so the client’s downward ray resolves onto its actual approach.

Eleven portals, seven interior destination spawns and fourteen secrets remain. Each secret has a physical prop beside an authored standing post and a short path spur. Existing keys, resources, combat roles and links are unchanged. The League Post-House’s returned mail and countersign story is preserved.

The shared north-road collar is centred at world `(190.5,34,-213.5)`, outward north, paired with Grey Moors’ west approach. It reserves a 40m half-width receiving strip and broad grading domain; civic doors remain outside that collar. The source calls the shared border API and exports the matching receiving-scene previews.

Evidence and final measurements belong to the generated validators and the coastal rollout review. Close camera views still show simple polygonal path edges and repeated masonry textures. Static geometry streams across the surveyed road; server actors/resources and handoff performance retain their existing limitations.
