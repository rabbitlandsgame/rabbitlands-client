# Sunmane Steppe coverage map

The concept boards remain the visual authority. This September 2026 pass keeps
the Orun canvas, timber, banners, warm grass and pale stone, and gives their
placement a coherent economy and travel route.

| Concept / place | Authored layout | Review view |
|---|---|---|
| Caravan road | Mirrorhold approach crosses the beck and faces the west gate; side inns leave the road clear | review-west-arrival |
| Round-tent camps | Four clan groups retain their shared market and separate horse grounds | p02-round-tent-camp |
| Seasonal market | Storage, crafting, training and information share the south court; story NPCs gather around the hall | review-service-court |
| Banner shrines | Clan shrines sit beside the camp paths and pasture crossings | p04-banner-shrine |
| Fortified approaches | Four gate openings carry the main roads; the northern route bends around the hall and barrows | p05-caravanserai-gate, review-north-road |
| Windmills and grain | Five grain plots sit on lower pasture ground; mill roads reach their yards | p06-windmill |
| Wells and pens | Wells serve the camps; six waterholes and the descending beck water the outer pasture | p07-well-and-pens, review-water-pasture |
| Standing stones | The pasture bridge rewards the stone-circle view; desert menhirs frame the northern crossing | p08-standing-stones, review-dune-road |
