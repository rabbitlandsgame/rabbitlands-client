# Sunmane Steppe change log

## September 2026 — caravan market, watered pasture and surveyed crossings

The market was a strong silhouette surrounded by roads that ignored its walls
and a stream whose bridge decks did not describe usable bank-to-bank routes.
The new road survey uses the four gate openings, bends around the hall and
barrows, and serves side courts at all four inns. Three shared timber spans
carry explicit walking surfaces between surveyed banks. The actual server
arrival stays at (79, 37), with a graded lane into the south court.

The beck descends past the western inn rather than through it. Waterholes are
clipped inside their terrain bowls with the new shared watercraft recipe.
Grain plots occupy lower watered pasture; pens and windmills sit beside their
working roads. The northern drovers camp moved off a steep shoulder onto the
sheltered salt-road floor. The hall's roof drums now close the gaps between its
canopies, preserving the existing silhouette and materials.

Server content follows the settlement: the market groups services, residents
stand at useful posts, and wildlife moves from pasture through outer breaks to
the badland. All 54 spawns and 18 species remain. Re-authoring also supplies the
13 later catalogue harvest nodes in named habitats. Every exterior departure
and authored content row is reachable from the primary arrival. Client marker
positions are recovered from stable server identities after each build;
resource display labels use short names.

Collision now samples the same building pads as the visible ground and
recognises world-space structural walls. Only declared walking decks reopen
water. The native grid retains 0.2 m height precision; the server grid now
encodes the real relief at 0.8 m per stage. The three correction passes and
crossing tests remain part of the rebuild contract.

The surface GLB grows from 19,237,080 to 19,249,384 bytes; LOD2 grows from
10,079,016 to 10,171,192 bytes. No material family or surface class is added.
See [layout-review.md](layout-review.md) for visual evidence, remaining legacy
surface contacts and the validation record.
