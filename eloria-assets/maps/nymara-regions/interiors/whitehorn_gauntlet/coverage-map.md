Both published bands and all three copies retain their enemies, counts,
variants, rules, rewards, keeper and exterior return berth. Instance bounds,
arrival, gates, interactives and encounter coordinates follow the rebuilt
geometry. The existing resource roster is preserved.

All nine gate approaches and destinations are walkable. The staging, legal
leg-to-leg routes, either fork, boss and vault pass the server's climb and
diagonal movement rules. Every one of the 4,007 walkable server tiles has an
exported Walk surface beneath its centre. Gate cuts and the surrounding void
remain blocked.
