# The Ice Stair — modeling assumptions

This is the abandoned silver stair inside Whitehorn's glacier. The exterior's
[concept detail board](../../whitehorn_range/references/00-concept-detail-board.png)
sets the palette: cold grey stone, blue ice, pale snow, dark timber braces,
silver ore and amber lamps. All geometry and textures use the shared procedural
toolkit, including the new frozen-cascade and worked-ore-face recipes. No
surface classes were inserted or renumbered.

The sheltered arrival holds ore sledges, stores and a haul winch, with a braced
frame pointing to the first gate. Worked ore faces explain the loading bays.
Winches sit at the upper stair landings, where the ore could be transferred.
The narrow suspension bridge meets stone abutments at both ends; its gentle
sag stays within the server's legal height changes. Lamps hang inward on arms
clear of the solid posts.

The fork presents its choice from the hub. Blue Crevasse follows glacier ice
between frozen falls and crystals; White Crevasse is a snowy, shored haul adit.
Paired nine-metre frozen wings frame the Rime Matriarch's court and its reward
doorway. A quarter-metre rear step completes the route from the raised dais
to that doorway.

Seven legs and both branches remain. Eight-metre gate passages remove 36 metres
of empty traversal, reducing the route footprint from 361 to 325 metres and
leaving 311 metres between arrival and vault. Working bays and frozen falls
break up views while keeping the combat floors and gate approaches clear.
The gauntlet progresses from staging through successive encounters to the
court; its existing difficulty bands and timed bridge rules remain intact.

Room lids use overhead nodes. Same-facing joint overlaps are clipped before
export. Local render batches keep point lights near the surfaces they light.
The sealed manifest has no sun, a cool ambient fill, warm mine lamps and blue
ice accents. Captures use that environment and the game's InteriorCutaway
controller. Darker void and sealed gate cuts are intentional.
