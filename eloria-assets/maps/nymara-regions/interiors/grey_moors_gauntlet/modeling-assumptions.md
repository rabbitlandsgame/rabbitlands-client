# The Barrow Run — modeling assumptions

The Grey Moors concept detail board is the visual source: wet granite,
drystone barrow faces, carved lintels, timber boardwalks over black water,
exposed dead roots and votive candles. The connected-family-barrows lore in
the gauntlet design supplies the layout. Geometry and textures remain
procedural recipes in the shared toolkit.

The arrival is a sheltered place to prepare and read the rules. Low
capstones lead into the Peat Cut, whose side chambers contain family cists
and the existing Moss node. The First Barrow and Fifth Chamber place graves
and offering niches beside the visitation lane; their centres retain room
for the published packs and party movement.

The Bog Board crosses a flooded peat chamber on close-set cross planks.
Bearers, driven piles and diagonal trestles reach below the deck to the
waterbed. Three braced lamp frames divide the crossing into short reaches.
A continuous substrate four centimetres below the plank tops catches
grounding rays in the joints. It never shares the planks' top plane.

The fork presents an intact, carved burial passage and a breached, rooted
peat passage. Both retain the existing gate choices and encounters. The
Reeve's Stair rises toward a larger family hall; its crown of split slabs
marks the final doorway. The rear dais step keeps the route to the reward
cache climbable.

Gate passages shorten from twelve to eight metres, removing thirty-six
metres of empty traversal while retaining seven legs, a fork, the timed
crossing and the court. This remains an instanced route; its safe arrival,
escalating encounters and two branch treatments take the place of an
exterior hub and danger rings.

New barrowcraft recipes are reusable and material-parameterised. Existing
material names and surface IDs are unchanged. Local mesh batches keep
point lights near their intended surfaces. Lids stay in add_overhead and
the package declares gameplay cutaways. The environment has no sun, a
muted cold ambient fill and warm lamps; captures use that actual manifest.

The package owns a geometry-derived walk grid. Do not apply the exterior
height-field corrections: the intentional gate cuts must stay sealed.
The server layout refresh moves published content onto authored candidates
and preserves rules, rosters, rewards, keeper coordinates and exterior returns.
