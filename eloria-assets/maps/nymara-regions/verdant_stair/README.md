# Verdant Stair — inhabited limestone terraces

Verdant is a **360 × 360 m** jungle region with a protected full-size arrival neighbourhood. Its origin is server (108,108); the six main flights rise from the working lagoon shore to a 62 m limestone summit. The jade temple, water shrine, cenote spiral, carved terrace houses, banyans and rope/root crossings retain their native architectural scale.

The composition follows a practical ascent: boat landing and quay houses; physick beds, provisioner and civic services; the Grand Stair and canopy settlement; spray-fed shrine pockets; the temple procession; quarry workers and the dry Steppe Pass. The Moved Anchor still rests on rollers beside its empty socket, preserving lore thread J. Plants follow the shaded shelves and damp gullies, with cleared views along the public flights.

## Current runtime package

`world.glb` and `world-lod2.glb` contain procedural geometry and embedded textures. `collision.bin` is a 720 × 720 half-metre grid. `minimap.webp` is rendered from the geometry. `world.json` declares all 45 original landmark IDs, 9 ordinary portals, 10 arrivals/returns and 13 exterior secret entrances; the provisioner's separate secret remains inside the seven-room insides map. Original concept references and earlier production captures remain under `references/` as historical evidence.

Current gameplay-camera before/after evidence and the regional route audit live in `work-output/southern-rollout/verdant-stair/` in the integration workspace. The live server fixture is `live-fixture.json`; its served standing posts are audited after paired publication, including actual NPC and furniture occupancy.

## Reproduction

```sh
python source/rebuild_landscape.py
python source/test_landscape_contract.py
```

The wrapper builds main/LOD/minimap/raw collision, then runs shared height refinement, exposed-deck opening, solid stamping, the actual tile-centre guard, and runtime verification. The guard closes unsupported or submerged actor positions at tile+.5. Server publication must preserve this authored mask; it must not reopen content posts or relax the physical terrain.

`source/landscape_plan.py` owns the compact survey: piecewise coordinate migration with the arrival neighbourhood kept at useful spacing, terrace heights, public routes, supported landings, village frontages, all 13 named resident posts and pool-water contours. Pools retain their connected spring catchments; the lower basin spills down into its original stream channels instead of flooding the public stair. `region.py`, `populate.py` and `build_verdant_stair.py` retain the native deterministic kit and seed 20260829. All meshes/textures are generated by the shared toolkit; no imported asset pack is required.

Before paired content synchronization, the integration coordinator invokes:

```sh
python source/migrate_compact_server.py --server /path/to/server --apply
```

The migration is revision guarded, covers returning portal destinations and content tables, and preserves interior coordinates. The source content layout requires full wildlife counts and anchors population to the main connected arrival region. Server and continent files are owned by integration, rather than by this regional wrapper.

## Preservation and practical checks

`preservation-contract.json` records the complete baseline landmark/portal/spawn ID sets. The regression test compares those sets, casts actual exported geometry across seven lanes of all six stair flights, checks that all flight lanes stay above the actual water, preserves clear resident/door separation, and checks the exact shared portal datums. The regional access audit folds raw authored collision with the server's normal height stages and uses `World.find_path`, with no synthetic bridges, content openings or step relaxation.

The old map remains visible in archived baseline evidence. Its broad tiled courts, isolated landings, repetitive canopy wall and cell-sized water notches informed this replan. Roads still have some polygonal material edges and foliage remains stylized. Actors/resources are map scoped, and cold-loading/handoff performance belongs to the combined live verification; the package alone does not prove a completely global seamless world.

## Editor kit

`source/prepare_meshy_kit.py --input <folder>` seats the generated models from
`work-output/verdant-stair-2026-09-27/asset-prompts.md` in the territory palette's
kit (`godot-client/world_authoring/regions/verdant_stair/assets/prototypes/kit-*.glb`):
mossy limestone crags and boulders, undergrowth, flowering shrubs, tree ferns and
saplings, mossy logs and stumps, wildflower meadow patches, river reeds and stones,
waterfall rocks, village props (lanterns, beehives, produce, drying racks, pots and
baskets), wayside jade shrines and beach pieces. Each is scaled to its size in
`SIZES`, stood on the origin, and its textures shrunk and content-addressed into
`assets/textures/`; `source/prepare-meshy-kit.json` records the input and output
digests and `--check` verifies them. Ground cover and small props start
walk-through in the palette by their names (`asset_catalog.gd` `WALK_THROUGH_WORDS`).
