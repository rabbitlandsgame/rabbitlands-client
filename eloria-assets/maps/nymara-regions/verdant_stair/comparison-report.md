# Verdant Stair: concept versus build — 2026-09-07

The comparison sheets now use Godot 4.7.2 GL Compatibility with the package's
manifest environment. The sun direction was corrected to illuminate the map
from above; the previous harness lighting had hidden the manifest error.

The six broad stair flights read from their axes and meet their shelves.
The lower market has a clear service court, the cenote has a visible approach,
and the two new ravine views show complete routes through the vegetation.
The diagonal climb from lagoon to temple remains the composition's backbone.

The concept still has denser architecture, flowering accents and a stronger
temple silhouette than the build. Large foliage cards, abrupt quarry and
bridge-bank cuts, and the canopy surrounding distant temple views remain
visible limitations. The improvement is strongest at the route and
settlement scale; the overall architectural density remains below the art.

See [the layout review](layout-review.md) for measured connectivity, final
validation, image links and remaining work.
