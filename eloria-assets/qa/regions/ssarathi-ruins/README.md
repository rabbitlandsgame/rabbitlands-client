# Ssarthi Ruins regional QA

Ssarthi Ruins now follows the concept's flooded ceremonial capital. The temple
overlooks a cross-shaped waterworks axis, with vault entrances, water gates,
sunken courts, ritual pools, solar stelae, and ruin arches defining the sacred
districts and submerged approaches.

The layout includes one temple, four vault entrances, six water gates, six
sunken courts, five ritual pools, eight sun stelae, and six ruin arches. Eight
teal landmark lights supplement the four transition lights. Dedicated terrain
separates temple ground, jungle ruins, ceremonial channels, and pools while
preserving the clear `(58,58)` arrival datum.
