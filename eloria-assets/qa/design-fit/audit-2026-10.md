# Equipment fit audit of the remaining sets (2026-10)

The owner asked for "a similar audit on other sets of armor using what we have
learned" after the Phoenix set and the four class starter kits were
design-fitted and reviewed four times in the viewer
([canonical-equipment-refit.md](../canonical-equipment-refit.md), "Set-level
design fit" onwards). This page audits every other generated set on the two
Human bodies (`luminous_male`, `luminous_female`), ranks the sets, and proposes
fix batches. The first batch, Sunmane 02, 03, 05 and 06, is design-fitted and
installed. Its results are in the QA doc, section "Audit batch 1: Sunmane
(2026-10-18)". After the owner's review of the audit sets (bent legs, boots
out of line with the trousers, trousers through the coats), the other 56 sets
were design-fitted in one pass: QA doc, section "Audit batch 2: every
remaining set (2026-10-18)". The headwear track below is still open.

## Scope

the theme's weapon and the Red Cape for the cape views. All 64 sets are now in

The 20 pieces already design-fitted (Phoenix, the four kits and their
headwear) are skipped. That leaves 60 sets with 244 pieces. The
legendary_eloria_boots (6:208-215) are measured only as the legendary sets'
alternative boots. Four sets are entirely done (militia 02, amberwood 06,
arcane 07, legendary 01) and serve as the reference below.

How often a set is worn is taken from its tier. Every item drops and can be
crafted, so the armour value (server `items.txt`) decides who wears it. The
Sunmane (0-1 per piece) and Frontier (0-1) lines are the starting tiers, then
Ranger, Amberwood and Militia (1-3), Arcane and Knightly (2-7), and Legendary
(6-9).

## Method

Each set was measured three ways, on both bodies, with the installed GLBs.

**Offline measures through the clips.** The installed pieces were skinned in
numpy with the body's own pose matrices (posekit, as the fitters pose it) at
33 frames: Idle_Subtle, 8 of Walk, 8 of Run_Female, 10 of Bow_Pull_Back and 6
of Fighting_Idle. Rays were cast through Blender BVH trees. Idle skinning of a
design-fit piece reproduces its solved shell to 0.1 mm (median), so the poses
are the client's. There is one measure per issue class the owner flagged:

| Class | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Trousers through the coat | Rays round the trunk axis below 1.15 m where both pieces exist: share on which the trouser art lies over the coat art, front and back (worst frame) |
| Collar in the nape | Idle: 1 cm × 10° cells behind the neck (1.40-1.62 m) where the coat art lies inside the skin, and how deep |
| Tail or back plates against the cape | Coat or trouser art behind the plane of the upper back (where a cape hangs plumb), 0.55-1.15 m, idle and Fighting_Idle |
| Legs-to-boots gap | Calf and ankle rays that meet neither trouser nor boot art, in idle, walk and run |
| Bulk | Coat standoff over the skin (p90, 1.05-1.42 m), thigh standoff (p90), upper-arm standoff (median) |
| Helm size | Median gap between helm and head over the upper hemisphere round the cranium centre |
| Collar-helm gap | Height of bare neck between the coat's top and the helm's lowest edge at the back. It matters for hoods, coifs and closed helms; a band or a hat shows the neck by design |
| Tearing | Share of art triangles stretched past 1.8× their idle length (the kits' tearing measure): Bow_Pull_Back for coats, every clip for legs and boots |
| Shards | The art vertex farthest from the body in idle, walk, run and the bow pull |
| Off the concept | Silhouette IoU against the set's concept target (below) |

**A concept target per set.** The Phoenix set and the kits were first
assembled in Blender from their raw pieces. Each set here was assembled the
same way: its raw `.glb.orig` pieces were carried onto the idle body with the
design-fit solver. The specs were drafted automatically, by rules calibrated
on the four kit specs, which they reproduce to within 0.04 of each girth scale:

- the torso's girth scale is the smallest that clears the chest by 35/18 mm
  and the waist by 18/12 mm (width/depth);
- the legs' scale is the smallest that clears the thigh by 37 mm and the knee,
  held to 0.85-1.0;
- the boots' scale comes from the foot length.

Where a skirt or a flap hides the crotch, 0.15 (source units) was assumed.
The legendary legs carry their sabatons in the source, which these rules do
not cover, so the legendary sets have no target here. They got Phoenix-style
specs in the second pass (QA doc, "Audit batch 2"). The silhouette IoU of the
installed fit against its target, front and side, is the "off concept" class.
The installed fits score 0.77-0.92 front and 0.61-0.86 side. The kits scored
0.75-0.89 before their design fit and 0.97-0.995 after it.

**The engine.** The review viewer's `--replay` shot 12 views per set and body
on the real actor: 1,440 shots, with no script errors. The views are idle
front, side and back, a low front view (groin and hems), a high back view
(shoulder tops, nape and scalp), Walk and Run_Female from the side,
Bow_Pull_Back from the front and the back, Fighting_Idle, and the cape from
the back and in Fighting_Idle. Four of them were shot again with
`--debug-tint yes` (480 shots), which draws linings magenta, reconstructed
inseams green and the body cyan, to tell a hole from a lining.

Contact sheets, one per theme (the concept target, then 8 engine views, male
and female per set):

## Findings

Almost every installed set shares these problems. They come from the
per-piece fitters:

- **Collars sit inside the nape.** This holds for 59 of the 60 sets: in idle a
  median of 127 neck cells, up to 6-10 cm deep (median 59 mm). The kits'
  `collar` fix only reached the design-fitted torsos.
- **Trousers come through the coat.** 39 sets do so at the back (over 30% of
  the covered rays in some frame) and 29 at the front (over 50%). Part of the
  front figure is drawn layering, such as a belt over a coat; the back is not.
- **Bare neck between collar and helm.** 47 sets show more than 2 cm at the
  back. Mostly the collar sits in the nape; bands and hats show the neck by
  design.
- **Helms read large.** 30 helms stand more than 75 mm off the head (median
  gap), and 14 more than 95 mm. The brims of the Riding, Plumed and Drover
  hats are part of that.
- **Gaps between trousers and boots.** 18 sets leave more than 2% of the calf
  rays with no art, and 9 more than 5% (Ranger 03 and 05 15%, Sunmane 06 9%).
- **Tails and plates behind the back.** 14 sets put coat or trouser art more
  than 2.5 cm behind the plane of the upper back, where the cape hangs.
- **Proportions off the concept.** Sleeves are scaled with the arm's length
  (the Splinted Vest's short sleeves became long bell sleeves). Tassets and
  skirts flare into bells (Sunmane 05 and 08, Legendary 06). Baggy trousers
  hide the boots (Militia 03, Sunmane).

Tearing is not a problem of the old fits. Their sleeves are bound with
torso_remap's arm field, and only 2 coats pass 1.5% in the bow pull. The kits'
tearing came with their design fit and was fixed in review; the Sunmane batch
uses those fixes (`bind_sleeves`, the shoulder seam blend).

## Proposed batches

Worst and most worn come first, about four sets each, the size of the kit
batch the owner reviewed. Pieces already design-fitted inside a set stay as
they are.

Batch 1 was done on its own. Batches 2-11 were then done together, because
the owner's review found bent legs and trousers through the coats on most of
the sets (QA doc, "Audit batch 2"). The notes on each batch below were checked
in that pass. The legendary_eloria_boots, which no set wears, were not refitted.

1. **Sunmane 02, 03, 05, 06**: done in this pass (see the QA doc).
2. **Sunmane 01, 04, 07, 08**: the rest of the starting-tier Steppe line, with
   the same rules. Watch the Sunplate Tassets' bell and the Corded Trousers'
   back flap.
3. **Frontier 03, 07, 06, 04**: starting tier. The female Patched Workshirt's
   back shard comes first (24 cm, through the cape).
4. **Ranger 07, 06, 01, 03**: chaps skirts and the 15% gaps between trousers
   and boots.
5. **Militia 03, 07, 08, 04**: the Splinted Vest's sleeves, and trousers over
   the greaves.
6. **Amberwood 03, 07, 08, 02**.
7. **Arcane 01, 06, 05, 04**: robes and tabards (`drape` for the tails).
8. **Frontier 05, 08, 01, 02 and Ranger 05, 08, 04, 02**. Ranger 04's legs
   and Ranger 02's torso are already done.
9. **Militia 01, 05, 06, Amberwood 01, 04, 05, Arcane 02, 03, 08**.
10. **Knightly 01-08**: mild, mostly trousers over the cuirass and collars.
11. **Legendary 02-08** and the legendary_eloria_boots: end-game gear. The legs
    carry sabatons, so each set needs a Phoenix-style spec read by hand.

Headwear is a separate track: `frame` rows in `headwear.json` for helm size,
`scalp: "show"` for open-faced helms once `refit_race_headwear.py measure`
shows no scalp poke, and cowls for hoods over their own set's collar. Each
headwear change refits 16 bodies, and the cowls read the torso, so headwear is
best done per theme after that theme's coats are design-fitted.

## What the audit does not cover

- Holes are judged from the tinted engine views, not measured.
- The cape class is a static proxy (the plane of the upper back). The live cape
  lifts in motion, so walk and run were not scored against it.
- Races other than the two Human bodies were not shot. Below the neck they wear
  the Human fits.
- The concept targets are automatic drafts. They are good enough to measure
  how far an installed fit is from its concept, but each batch still reads its
  skeleton off the gridded renders before fitting. The first batch needed
  three hand readings and two new spec keys.

## Reproduction

Scripts and data are in the client's ignored
`.local/asset-authoring/working/equipment-audit-2026-10-18/` (`scripts/`,
`fit/`):

- `make_sets.py` groups the sets; `viewer_sets.py` writes the viewer's
  "Audit" sets.
- `pose_export.py` (Blender) exports the 33 body poses; `metrics.py`
  (Blender, about 3 s per set and body) writes the measures; `summarize.py`
  and `rank.py` build the ranking; `audit_table.py` writes the table above.
- `fit/survey.py`, `fit/analyze.py`, `fit/auto_readings.py` (with
  `overrides.json`), `fit/make_specs.py` and `fit/run_df.sh` draft and solve
  the concept targets; `fit/review_all.sh` renders each target against the
  installed fit, and `fit/iou.py` scores them.
- `make_views.py` and `engine.sh` shoot the viewer views; `sheets.py` and
  `compare.py` build the sheets.
- Second pass: `legtilt.py` measures the leg line against the targets,
  `post_refit2.sh` measures and shoots the candidates, `final_table.py`
  writes the QA doc's tables, and `evidence2.py` and `evidence3.py` build the
  before/after sheets. `fit/dbg_split.py` (Blender) dumps a solve's limb split;
  `fit/split_rules.py`, `fit/split_plot.py` and `scripts/farverts.py` trace
  far bow-pull vertices to it.
