# A Daughter in the Dark

The rescue begins the first time an eligible player leaves Landfall by the south ramp, provided another tracked quest is not blocking the area event. Help Elen immediately, or heal her after watching the ambush. You can agree to help or return after preparing.

Elen travels with the rescue party and supports it with a finite supply of healing ether. Stay together, watch her resources and use Heal Target if she falls. The quest follows the normal tracking, Hide, Untrack and replay controls.

Follow the lanterns through Lantern Mine's Upper Galleries to the stairs and the lower workings. The stairs remain available while ordinary goblin groups survive. Secure the captive's chamber, free Liora and escort the family outside before choosing your personal rescue reward.

## Check in with Liora

After completing the rescue, track **Check in with Liora** in the quest journal. Elen and Liora are staying at Landfall's existing inn near Hob Tennant. The quest marker points to the inn outdoors and to Liora inside. Walk there and speak with either family member when ready.

The visit is a short portrait conversation. Ask what Liora remembers, or reassure her that she does not have to talk about it yet. Either answer completes the check-in; there is no wrong answer or combat requirement. Each player chooses for their own character, including when visiting with a party.

The visit does not pay the rescue reward again or replace another quest assignment. Existing completed rescuers can make the same visit. Replays show the recorded answer without changing it, and the follow-up is archived after completion.
