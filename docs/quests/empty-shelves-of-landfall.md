# The Empty Shelves of Landfall

Ilka Tarn's shop, The True Note, has tools and empty essence shelves. The player restores the people, gathering stations and roads that supply it, then helps the villagers produce their own essences. Each restored supply permanently opens that essence family for the individual player.

Fire and Water can be restored in either order. After the teaching chapter, Wind and Magic can be restored in either order; Death and Frost then form another pair. Both members of each pair are required before the next major chapter.

For the eight essence supply chapters, the direct route adds 150 Magic XP and 100 gold. The careful route adds 200 Alchemy XP and ten essences of that chapter's family. The conversation shows this extra reward before the player chooses.

## Playing together and recovering

One to eight players can take an expedition together through their normal party. Shared fights, repairs and deliveries count for the expedition; each learner demonstrates their own casts, gathering and mixtures. Unlocks and rewards are personal and paid once. Completed players can help another party member without receiving the same chapter reward again.

Mara Vale accompanies field work as a novice Warden healer, using real healing spells and finite ether. Leaving saves the expedition, cart and surviving enemies' health. A returning learner can finish personal practice after other members have collected their rewards.

Supply chests use normal personal storage. Gatherers' caches beside the resource patches let players bank materials and mix from storage. Crops not yet native to Landfall arrive in the crew's cultivation trays. A genuine critical mixing failure permits replacement of the lost training ingredients; repeated requests alone do not generate supplies.

The intact gland has a chance to drop for nearby participants, with a usable gland guaranteed by their twelfth eligible spider. A late learner can request another nest after the first is cleared. Ordinary enriched-mix chances remain unchanged.

## Presentation and follow-on work

Conversations use the shared cinematic dialogue window, headshots and gold speaker names. The camera settles on the conversation; field reviews and apprentice lessons retain the gameplay view. Choices occupy the same dialogue frame with the player's portrait. Return to Play stops at an unanswered decision, and replay displays the recorded selection without changing progress.

The shop keeps locked essence rows visible as **Out of stock**. Restored stock is personal and permanent. Other merchants and essences already owned or traded remain usable.

After restoring Frost stock, players with Potion level 16 can practise three Frost Essence mixes using the existing Reed-and-Salt recipe. Ilka supplies the ingredients; the once-only review awards 1,500 Potion XP, 500 Alchemy XP and one Enriched Frost Essence. The higher-level Silverleaf recipe also counts. This assignment is optional and does not block the main series.

The finale opens one standing supply contract per day: deliver 100 basic essences for 600 gold (4,200 for Frost), 250 Alchemy XP and 100 Magic XP. Ingredient bundles remain available for the restored families.

The implementation has a complete automated gameplay run and native portrait renders. Human playtesting is still needed to judge expedition duration, combat difficulty and the staging of the existing field props; the chapter bonuses and objective counts are tuning values.
