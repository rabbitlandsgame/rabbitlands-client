# The Empty Shelves of Landfall

Ilka Tarn's shop, The True Note, has tools and empty essence shelves. The player learns to gather, mix and use magic, then protects the people who supply the shop. Practice batches do not open merchant stock. Life Essence becomes available after the Old Warren raid. The Price of a Spark restores Fire through quarry recovery, Coal extraction and a sapper confrontation. Where the Water Goes restores Water through coastal sluices, cutter protection, a barge escort and the upper gatehouse.

Ilka gives a once-only allowance of 350 Fire, 150 Life and 250 Water Essences, plus 20 minor healing potions, when assigning Blackcut. Overflow goes to personal storage. Shop stock remains locked until the final report. The four level-10 workers recover sealed merchant stock and mine the remaining resources using native harvesting rates. Their shipment never enters the player's inventory. The quest window distinguishes recovered and newly mined secured loads, carried goods and worker health.

Workers walk between the resource patches, stock bay and shipment store. The lift route needs a living mechanic and three protected repairs; the old passage uses a longer cart route. Heal Target revives downed workers during setup as well as extraction. Pausing preserves worker health, cargo, secured loads and finished preparations. Defeat drops unsecured bags and offers Retry/Cancel while retaining secured loads. No offline yields are produced.

Krek and his guards are present from the start. The signal stops reinforcement waves; water reduces blast size and damage; the powder fuse or a paid Fire Bolt on its native barrel target damages Krek once. Four-second ground warnings match the actual circular blast areas. Ordinary combat experience remains normal. Fire stays locked through the first two reports and opens only after Krek is defeated and Ilka hears the report. Water remains locked.

Assignments mark the real worksites; players walk there and choose when to begin. Party helpers keep their own unfinished quests and receive no duplicate allowances or rewards. Portrait conversations combine consecutive lines from the same speaker, retain one camera framing, and show the recorded choice on replay. Reports hand directly into the next briefing. The final report closes on Continue. Public-world restoration keeps the player's current position rather than sending them back to the worksite entrance.

The target is roughly 50–75 minutes including travel and combat, subject to a full-length pacing playtest. Native automated journeys accelerate quantities and clocks while retaining real movement, spell costs, creature deaths and reward validation.

The cargo boat follows a connected route through three landings while players use the adjacent towpath. It waits if the party falls behind. Ada must be standing to pilot it. The party copies reuse Landfall's existing terrain, collision, streaming and map preparation. Approved gates, jetties, gathering plants and a cargo-carrying boat dress the worksite; the existing flow-effect renderer makes channels and the accepted boat passage visible. Public worksites are marked only for the assigned expedition. Native bandits use approved Human bodies and invasion colors, with ordinary combat experience.

Veyra has 500 health and is present from the start. Closing either guard gate is optional; no offensive school is required. After her defeat the gates reopen and the surviving cutters walk through during a settled closing conversation. Only Ilka's final report opens Water stock. Every reward is personal and paid once; helpers keep their own quest progress. Portrait choices, read-only replay, immediate Continue and seamless report-to-briefing handoffs use the normal quest controls. Full-duration pacing and novice balance remain playtest tuning tasks.

### Protecting the crew

Fenna, Sela, Nell and Ada have Harvesting 40 and gather Sage and Hearthroot once every 0.25 seconds per worker, carrying 250 items per trip. The quest window separately shows gathered items, items in bags and items delivered to the crew store. Only deliveries complete a shipment. The cargo belongs to Ilka and never enters the player's tradable inventory. Personal storage remains separate.

The four workers have visible health bars and companion rows beside the human party. Downed workers stop working until a successful Heal Target cast revives them. A story-owner defeat resets the current field and offers Retry or Cancel; a completed Sage shipment survives a later Hearthroot defeat. Pausing or disconnecting saves progress and worker health without offline production. Gathering swings are four times faster than at level 10; hauling and combat still add to completion time.

Ilka provides one guard allowance: 1,500 Fire, 250 Life and 500 Water Essences, plus 20 minor healing potions. Items that do not fit go to personal storage. Restarting or canceling does not issue another allowance.

## Playing together and recovering

Ilka assigns each chapter at The True Note and gives directions in her conversation. Accepting an assignment keeps the player at the shop. The quest instructions list the destination and tasks; **Do now · Show on map** opens the map without moving the player. The normal Instructions, minimize, Hide and Untrack controls remain available, and the assignment stays in the quest log.

A tracked assignment adds a gold **!** to the map and minimap. Inside the shop, it marks the correct door; outdoors, it marks the worksite. Walk there and use the labelled start interaction or the quest card's **Start** button when ready. Raids, spider hunts and delivery escorts begin at their worksite. Walking past a marker alone does not force a cinematic or encounter. Nearby party members enter by travelling there too.

Fenna introduces the crew at the Sage beds. The camera establishes one view of the group and stays settled during the conversation; later field dialogue keeps the gameplay view. Ilka stays in her shop. After the Hearthroot shipment, players return to The True Note. Her reward conversation continues directly into the mine assignment. Accepting the raid leaves the player free to walk there or untrack it.

One to eight players can take an expedition together through their normal party. Shared fights, repairs and deliveries count for the expedition; each learner demonstrates their own casts, gathering and mixtures. Unlocks and rewards are personal and paid once. Completed players can help another party member without receiving the same chapter reward again.

Friends can help protect the crew or raid the Warren even while their own earlier lessons remain unfinished. Their previous assignment resumes when they leave. Eligible learners each receive Ilka's guard allowance once; helpers receive no duplicate allowance or chapter reward.

The new raid uses a party-only copy of the existing Old Warren caves, not Lantern Mine. The ordinary entrance starts an accepted raid, subject to the normal tracked-quest area-start rules. Grask and his guards are present from the beginning; the boss chamber has no kill-count gate. Leaving saves defeated enemies and surviving enemy health. The retained later expeditions continue to use Mara's finite-mana Warden support.

Supply chests use normal personal storage. Gatherers' caches beside the resource patches let players bank materials and mix from storage. Crops not yet native to Landfall arrive in the crew's cultivation trays. A genuine critical mixing failure permits replacement of the lost training ingredients; repeated requests alone do not generate supplies.

The intact gland has a chance to drop for nearby participants, with a usable gland guaranteed by their twelfth eligible spider. A late learner can request another nest after the first is cleared. Ordinary enriched-mix chances remain unchanged.

## Presentation and follow-on work

Conversations use the shared cinematic dialogue window, headshots and gold speaker names. The camera settles on the conversation; field reviews and apprentice lessons retain the gameplay view. Choices occupy the same dialogue frame with the player's portrait. Return to Play stops at an unanswered decision, and replay displays the recorded selection without changing progress.

The shop keeps locked essence rows visible as **Out of stock**. Stock unlocks are personal. A previously earned Life supply from the full old field quest remains earned; introductory practice deliveries alone do not qualify. Other merchants and essences already owned or traded remain usable.

After restoring Frost stock, players with Potion level 16 can practise three Frost Essence mixes using the existing Reed-and-Salt recipe. Ilka supplies the ingredients; the once-only review awards 1,500 Potion XP, 500 Alchemy XP and one Enriched Frost Essence. The higher-level Silverleaf recipe also counts. This assignment is optional and does not block the main series.

The finale opens one standing supply contract per day: deliver 100 basic essences for 600 gold (4,200 for Frost), 250 Alchemy XP and 100 Magic XP. Ingredient bundles remain available for the restored families.

Native automated checks cover both deliveries, worker revival, party joins, pause/resume, retry choices, the cave entrance, raid combat and the Life unlock. Delivery counts and clocks are accelerated in progression tests; the production goals and actual level-40 swing timing are checked separately. Full-length combat pacing remains a playtest tuning task.
