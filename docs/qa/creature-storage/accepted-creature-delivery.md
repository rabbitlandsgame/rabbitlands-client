# Accepted creature bake installation

The five subsequently accepted comment repairs are installed as well. Their [delivery registry](accepted-repair-delivery.json) supersedes overlapping earlier selections, including Heartwood Treant. Those five runtime files fell from 97,614,044 to 5,992,016 bytes; see the [repair findings and measured CPU costs](comment-repair-batch-01.md). Earlier receipts below retain the installation history.

On 2026-10-28, 191 exact reviewed 30 fps, zero-corrective GLBs replaced their prior models in the creature-storage task checkout. This includes 89 button approvals, one explicit acceptance in a saved comment, and the 101 later close-alternative bakes separately authorized by the user's current instruction. Removing an entry from the review queue alone was not treated as installation approval.

The files are ordinary uncompressed GLBs. No approved payload was refitted or re-encoded during installation. All native clips, durations and loop policies match their bound evidence; every installed hash, 30 fps import policy and active roster identity was checked afterward. Fourteen unapproved models and the already accepted Crystalback Tortoise remained unchanged. Thirteen of those held models have repair comments; Two Headed Giant has no exact-version approval.

Fifteen accepted versions were previously local-only animation drafts. Their qualified owner-stage maps, native playback speeds, measured stride values, attachment aliases and clip policies were transferred into dedicated maps. Their import scale, facing and backset exactly match the configurations used during review. Shared generic maps were preserved. Existing semantic attachment configurations on the other 176 actors remain unchanged. The native asset catalog's current structural counts were refreshed for the installed versions.

The [accepted delivery registry](visually-accepted-delivery.json) is authoritative for these exact installed hashes. Earlier close-match selections remain documented in [runtime-delivery.json](runtime-delivery.json), with overlapping entries marked as superseded. Original authoring observations, profiles and physical witnesses were retained. Fresh Blender and actual raw-client captures bind the installed bytes; older captures explicitly cover direct poses and first-tick transitions, not the subsequently added frame-stepped crossfade checks.

Saved repair notes remain separate from visual acceptance. A pre-existing tear does not revoke acceptance of an otherwise accepted storage alternative. New repairs receive new hashes and comparisons. The repair pass begins with Skeleton, Tengu, Giant Wasp, Heartwood Treant and Nightweave Spider; position differences rank reference variance and are not by themselves a measure of how severe a pre-existing defect looks.

The guarded installer is `eloria-assets/tools/install_accepted_creature_bakes.py`. Its default mode validates a plan; `--install` applies it in a task worktree with staged replacements, backups, immediate hash guards and rollback. Recovery copies, the original review journal and per-file before/after hashes remain ignored locally. No push, release or primary-checkout refresh is included.
