# Playtest creature gaits

Skeleton, Zombie and Cerberus Walk and Jog are rebuilt on the current imported rigs. The original binary payload, geometry, rest, skins, materials, textures, corrective shapes and five other animation objects are preserved. `manifest.json` and the per-actor fit/audit reports bind the exact runtime files and cadence to their source hashes.

The fitter uses the physical weighted sole surfaces, two-bone leg solves and source upper-body/corrective motion at the matching cycle phase. It exports 192-Hz curves, with dense exported-skin contact sampling at 768 Hz or higher. Biped Walk is 1.12 seconds and Jog is 0.72 seconds. Cerberus retains its original durations and uses four-beat walking and diagonal jogging contacts. Runtime cadence follows the fitted native ground speed multiplied by the registry import scale; the server's actor scale remains a separate multiplier.

Fresh Blender imports compare 35 complete deformed poses per actor with the native evaluator, including all seven clips. Maximum disagreement is below seven micrometres in native units across the four actors. Front, rear, side and game views and translating 24-fps Godot sequences were reviewed. Skeleton's detached bone surfaces retain visible joint spacing in close views. Cerberus retains its thick original leg skin: maximum Jog edge-length excess is 0.050 native units, versus 0.024 in the original. These limits are recorded in the manifest.

Black Iron Death Knight retains its existing seven clips. Its dedicated animation map matches measured native ground speeds of approximately 0.47 and 1.05 units per second. The retained audit measures the actual weighted boot surfaces, including toe bones and the separate rigid sword. Central support intervals keep boot bottoms within 0.7–2.0 mm of the ground, with p95 sole drift below 0.028 native units per second. Lift and landing transitions are excluded from that contact statistic. Existing armour and cape deformation is retained; this does not certify rigid armour or self-collision. Its exact hash, contact measurements and fresh-import comparison are in the two `death-knight-retained` reports.

Rebuild candidates with `eloria-assets/tools/fit_undead_gaits.py`, audit with `audit_undead_gaits.py`, and run the native `tests/integration/rendered_undead_gaits.gd` fixture. Its default renders all four shipped rigs at ordinary travel speeds. Versioned originals, candidates and visual evidence remain under the ignored asset-authoring workspace.
