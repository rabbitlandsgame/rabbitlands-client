# Gauntlet loot ledger

The dense ledger keeps item quantities, recipients, assigned quantities and
per-item party prices together. Click an item icon to inspect it. Rare drops
use a diamond marker and an accent color. Personal cache rewards remain locked
to their owner.

Search, item-type filters, assignment filters and Rare only combine across the
entire run. Sort by run order, name, item type, quantity, item price or NPC unit
value. Filtering and sorting happen before pagination. The assignment summary,
storage previews and delivery eligibility always describe the whole run,
including items hidden by filters. Empty search results do not mean that all
loot has been assigned.

The leader can set a Gold Coin price per item on shared non-currency drops,
including ordinary items assigned to an individual player. Zero means free.
Recipients pay for their allocated units from storage; that payment is shared
equally among the original party, including the buyer. The buyer must have the
full charge available and approve the current plan before delivery. Changing
the price or allocation invalidates previous approval. Gold Coins and personal
cache rewards cannot be priced. NPC buyback prices remain server-controlled.

Use **Storage deliveries** to review everyone's complete storage preview.
**Split common (whole run)** and **More actions (whole run)** apply across all
pages, regardless of filters. Sales and exchange listings remain staged until
the existing final confirmation. **Review & send** preserves the storage
delivery and return-to-beam flow.

Whole-run browsing and ordinary-item pricing require the matching server
update. Older servers keep the existing ledger usable; unsupported browser
controls are disabled rather than filtering only the current page.
