# Local HUD sandbox

Double-click `test-hud-editor.bat` in the client checkout. It opens the real client HUD with a sample Landfall scene; it needs no login or server. Game connections, outgoing game commands, reconnects and update downloads are disabled for this process. Settings live in a separate local test profile and survive reopening.

The floating **Local HUD sandbox** box selects any of the five presets or Current HUD. **Edit HUD** opens the editor. **Restore preset default** restores the selected built-in. Drag either box by its title or fold the sandbox controls out of the way.

Use **Add member**, **Remove selected**, **Clear party** and **Reset samples** to test zero to twelve sample members. Select a member to toggle their offline appearance or cycle their health, including the low-health state. These controls remain usable while the HUD editor is open. **Show party roster in this preset** reveals the roster even when a preset normally hides it.

## Resizing and docking

- Drag a selected window's left/right handle to change its width, its top/bottom handle to change its height, or its corner to change both. The resource circle stays round.
- Resizing near another visible window's edge snaps the dragged dimension into alignment. Cyan guides identify the matching window. Both dimensions can snap during a corner drag. Hold **Shift** to bypass snapping, or switch **Snap resize edges** off in Layout. Undo reverses the whole gesture.
- Menu, item and spell areas reflow their actual controls to match the new aspect ratio. Their Layout tab also provides explicit **Rows**, **Columns**, **Window width** and **Window height**, plus a reflow switch. Icons retain their own proportions inside the cells. Every existing slot assignment and keyboard binding remains attached to its original button.
- Fixed widgets such as the clock and skill statistics also have separate width/height scales.
- Dock to another component using **Start**, **Center**, **End** or **Stretch** alignment. Start/end mean top/bottom for a left/right connection and left/right for a top/bottom connection. **Extend to screen edge** fills the remaining space on that side.
- **Dock to screen** pins a component to a side or corner with no forced outer margin. Dragging near an edge also previews a screen dock. Drag moves a connected group; Alt-drag detaches one component.
- In Look, **Show resource icons** toggles the health, Ether, food, action, load and XP emblems independently of labels and values.
- Select **Blank HUD panel** to place, resize, color or dock an empty backdrop. **Fill screen height** keeps the Heritage right rail flush with the screen; dragging a height handle releases that fit. **Hide while skill statistics visible** makes it a filler when those statistics are hidden. Outside the editor, the panel lets game clicks through. Option 1 keeps this backdrop visible with statistics enabled; the optional automatic-hide switch is off by default.
- Resource strips grow enough to hold every enabled icon, label, number and bar. Narrow strips reflow into more rows, including when bottom-docked. This fixes the old 22-pixel XP-strip size clipping ordinary resources below the screen.
- **Background opacity** and **Border opacity** in Look make transparency explicit. Enabling an old zero-opacity frame or choosing a new frame color restores visible opacity. Previously saved enabled panels with inherited zero-opacity XP colors are repaired once; newly chosen transparency is retained.
- **HUD background** and **HUD border** are independent switches in Look, including for the clock and compass. Panels joined without a gap share their outside frame; partial joins keep the exposed part of the border. Each panel retains its chosen color. Chat retains its native backdrop opacity and idle fade.
- **HUD border design** in Look offers **Plain**, **Beveled** and **Ornate**. The latter two use the concept's layered brass edges, with corner details or circular studs. Changing border color also recolors the trim. Decoration follows the outside of joined panels.
- **Compass → Look → Outside compass circle** selects **Transparent**, **Fill when docked**, or **Filled**. Filled corners make a rectangular HUD panel that joins seamlessly to the clock or other docked panels. Transparent corners keep just the circular instrument. Automatic fill recognizes attached children and screen docking too.
- **Minimap → Look → Minimap frame** selects **Match map**, **Circle** or **Rectangle**. Circle clips the map and keeps its border circular during resizing, scaling and docking. A round map can also sit inside a rectangular HUD panel. The original minimap shape menu updates the frame when Match map is selected.
- **Minimap → Look** also offers **Map name above minimap**, **Map directions** (All, North, None), **North marker** (Letter, Arrow), and **Direction distance from radius**. Positive distances move the annotations outside the radius; negative distances move them inside. The arrowhead tracks north as the live map rotates. **Map label HUD background / border** adds plaques using the minimap's background/border switches and colors. The map ring itself uses the separate **HUD border** switch.
- The party roster stays open when Escape closes other windows. Its close button and party toggle still work.
- Circle docking uses the visible ornament in either direction: attach a bar to the circle or attach the circle to a meter. Left/right and top/bottom joins draw matching backgrounds and remove only the shared section of border. Where perpendicular panels meet around the circle, their backgrounds also fill the enclosed corners. Joined translucent backgrounds are painted once; hiding the supporting background restores the open circular shoulders.
- Select **Resource meters → Options** to enable resources, move them earlier/later with the arrow buttons, and choose each bar's color. Its emblem uses the same color, including in the detailed player summary. Disabled resources retain their position in the order.
- **Quick spell count** (1–12) and **Quick item count** (1–8) remain visible on every editor tab when that bar is selected. Counts change the real controls and preserve their assignments and shortcuts.
- **Quick spells / Quick items → Look → Slot border** selects **Match HUD**, **None**, **Beveled** or **Ornate**, with a separate **Slot border color**. This trims individual icons while the overall HUD bar can remain floating. Borders remain visible on empty, unavailable and cooling item slots without enabling those slots.
- **Icon opacity** controls quick-slot artwork independently of the window background. Magic and skill icons now default to 100%, including unavailable spells and techniques. **Unavailable icon opacity** on the spell bar optionally applies additional fading relative to the main icon opacity. Availability reasons, cooldown tracking, badges, shortcuts and cast validation retain their behavior. These choices save in custom and remembered built-in layouts.
- **Game clock → Options → Clock display** selects the original live dial, digital clock, or both. Hiding the clock retains the selected mode for when it is enabled again.
- **Party roster → Options → Detailed player summary** shows your portrait initials, party-leader status, and five live resource bars with numbers. **Include yourself in roster** controls this summary too. Option 2 uses the detailed view when its roster is enabled. The sandbox's health cycling updates your own live summary when you select yourself.

If a saved test profile already contains an older layout, select the updated built-in (**Heritage Polish · option 1**, **Refined Classic · option 2**, **Painterly Adventure · option 4**, or **Minimal Modern · option 5**) and use **Restore preset default** to see these updated defaults. Existing saved custom layouts are retained.

Each built-in option now retains its own edits when you switch between options. **Done & save** saves those arrangements together. **Restore default** resets only the selected option, with Undo and Cancel available. **Save as new** still creates a separate named custom preset. Older profiles supply their currently active layout; layouts discarded by the earlier single-layout behavior cannot be reconstructed automatically.

Undo/Redo, custom preset saving, Cancel and Restore default remain available. Restoring a built-in keeps custom presets, both chat feeds, draft text and private-message recipients.

If the default graphics mode does not start on the machine, run `test-hud-editor.bat --compatibility`. `test-hud-editor.bat --check` runs the automated HUD checks without opening the interactive scene.
