# Spell potency

Eligible spells no longer randomly fizzle. A valid cast pays its quoted ether
and materials. Missing knowledge, level, Nexus, resources or a legal target
still prevent casting; rejection and cancellation spend nothing.

Selected Power 1–10 remains a separate investment. Magic Nexus and the existing
eight-Magic-level steps still gate higher power. Advanced families have earlier
access to Power 1 without moving their former thresholds for additional power.
Magic Offense still supplies hostile strength; Magic Defense supplies support.

Each spell has an unlock and recommended Magic level. Average potency reaches
100% at recommendation and 200% at twice recommendation, then stops increasing.
This multiplier applies to the existing strength calculation, so Magic level,
attributes, selected power and target mitigation continue to matter.

Advanced families are Ether Drain, Reveal, Disrupt, Elemental Ward and Magic
Immunity, including their delivery variants. They unlock at Magic 40 and have
recommended level 80. Other spells retain their unlock levels; recommendation
is the largest of 10, twice unlock, and unlock plus 10.

For an advanced damaging spell:

| Magic level | Average potency | Allowed range |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 40 | 60% | 36–84% |
| 60 | 80% | 62–98% |
| 80 | 100% | 88–112% |
| 120 | 150% | 138–162% |
| 160 and above | 200% | 188–212% |

The center follows a smooth curve between milestones. Results follow a bounded
bell curve rather than a uniform roll. Starter spells begin at 90% average;
heals and regeneration have one-third of the damage spread. Costs and XP never
depend on the potency roll. One roll covers a cast's recipients and later ticks.

Wards, Shield, Haste, Conceal and Magic Immunity scale duration predictably,
leaving their protective magnitude or binary effect intact. Other numeric
support and weakening effects scale magnitude predictably. Restore Ether scales
its restoration amount predictably over the existing channel duration.
Blink, Recall, Reveal, Dispel and Transmute retain fixed utility outcomes.
Their recommendation describes familiarity, not a random or doubled destination,
currency yield, dispel chance or detection result.

The spellbook shows unlock and recommended levels and the server's current
average and bounds. “200%” describes the center, not a hard upper bound on an
individual damage or healing roll. Selected Power remains unchanged by rolls.

Consumable materials and charged-focus substitution still use their existing
rules while alternative resource systems are being considered.
