# Essences

Rabbit Lands has eight essence families: Life, Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Death,
Magic and Frost. Earth covers the former Matter essence, Wind combines Air and
Spirit, and Fire covers the former Energy essence. Existing holdings combine
when the character database upgrades. Recipes and spells use the surviving
families; combined ingredient requirements add together.

Every family has an enriched counterpart. A successful mix of a base essence
has an unmodified **1 in 10,000 (0.01%)** chance to produce one enriched essence
in place of the ordinary result. Failed mixes do not roll an enriched result.
Mixing skill affects ordinary crafting success; it does not change this base
enrichment rate. The Artificer perk and configured world modifiers can change
the rare-result chance. Enriched essences are separate items and are never
automatically spent in place of ordinary spell or recipe reagents.

The base artwork retains its existing shapes and colors. Wind uses the former
Air painting; Earth and Fire keep their selected paintings. Enriched icons
preserve those silhouettes with brighter cores and luminous highlights.
