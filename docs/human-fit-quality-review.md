Ferris, Kethan, Osric, Cerys, Zadie, Lucan, Edda, Adira, Bastien, Veda,
Lenora and Ewan. The other starting looks retain their saved appearance.

short curls, shoulder curls, layered waves, curly waves, twin braids, a neat

existing hair-finish control. Original position groups are kept coherent so
control changes do not pull apart UV seams. Separate fine strands follow

beard and a close scalp-coloured fade. Shared approved beard assets were not
changed. Existing cornrow scalp channels now receive the actor's skin colour.

texture and numerical evidence by SHA-256. Head clearance and scalp coverage

cut, replacement skin and shirt. Fresh GLB-only imports and actual saved-look
client captures are separate visual gates. Removing Ferris's disconnected
nape remnants preserves exact retained geometry and fields; inherited checks
are labelled as subset proofs rather than new full scans.
