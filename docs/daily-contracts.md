# Landfall Daily Contracts

Speak with **Batu Ensh at the Landfall watch house** and choose **Landfall daily contracts**. You can also open **Contracts** from the quest journal to browse your assignments while traveling. Accepting work, collecting rewards, and exchanging service marks require returning to Batu.

## Pick your work

Batu posts **two offers per discipline at a time**: one Routine assignment and one higher-difficulty assignment that rotates with the UTC day. Select a skill using the radio buttons on the Available tab to load that skill’s two offers. Each card shows its name, description, and starting location, with buttons to accept or replace it. Contract cards do not list rewards or XP. Changing pages keeps your accepted work and tracked quest. The Accepted tab shows your assignments; the Marks tab lets you exchange service marks for supplies.

The board presents the offers as two pinned parchment notices with a teal header and a wooden frame. On narrow windows, the notices stack in a scrollable list. Use the header to move the window and the close button to return to play.

The full rotation draws from five assignments per discipline, covering Routine, Standard, Demanding, Hard, and Expert difficulty. The possible assignments are listed below; they are not all posted on the same day.

| Discipline | Assignments | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melee | The Watch on the Road; The Unbroken Perimeter; Clearing the Approaches; The Relief Column; The Thornline Breakthrough | Escort the repair crew, hold its camp against changing approaches, or clear hostile posts. Make your own melee contribution. |
| Magic | The Warden’s Call; Triage at the Watch; Breaking the Siege; The Arcane Perimeter; The Wardens’ Last Mile | Escort wounded wardens, restore real health at the recovery camp, or clear threat posts with magic. Healing a full-health character does not count. |
| Ranging | Towpath Lookout; The Distant Watch; Along the Ridgeline; The Longshot Convoy; The Ridgebreak Hunt | Cover a moving crew, defend the lookout, or clear dispersed ambushers. Bring an equipped, compatible bow and ammunition. |
| Summoning | A Watchful Companion; Guardians of the Supply; The Burrow Hunt; The Companions’ Vigil; The Packbreak Patrol | Escort the patrol, defend a supply camp, or clear hostile posts using your own summon. Learn a usable summon first. |
| Harvesting | The Gatherer’s Round; The Apothecary’s Basket; A Gatherer’s Survey; Supplies for the Watch; A Survey Beyond the Road | Gather one fresh resource, assemble a two-resource bundle, or gather a quota while sampling distinct nodes. Bring a Pickaxe for mining terms. |
| Alchemy | The Apprentice’s Order; From Reagent to Essence; A Shelf Ready for Sale; The Apothecary’s Reserve; The Guild’s Commission | Mix fresh batches, gather a fresh ingredient before mixing, or mix and deliver ordinary essences for the shelves. Bring a learned recipe and your Mortar and Pestle. |

Difficulty sets the amount of work and the threats you face. Combat assignments have three stages on Routine, Standard, and Demanding; four on Hard; and five on Expert. After accepting, check the quest tracker and Instructions for exact quotas and progress.

Protection assignments now name the route, camp, or hostile posts you will secure. Moving crews travel between distinct checkpoints; camp defenses stay in one place and face attacks from different approaches. Standard and Bold routes can take different paths. **The Watch on the Road** begins on Landfall’s **Eastwatch road**, away from the Sage harvesters.

Higher-difficulty work can take you to other regions. Check the named destination before setting out. Your map shows the assignment marker in its region and the next doorway or crossing when you need to travel there. Previously accepted assignments keep their saved destination and route.

Batu’s short briefing describes the accepted mission. Worksite conversations use the same portraits and highlighted speaker names, keeping your current camera view. Continue is available immediately, and Replay lets you review the briefing without changing quest progress.

You can accept up to **three contracts** and track one at a time. Accepted objectives and rewards stay fixed if your level changes. The marked destination and Instructions explain where to go. Walk there yourself, then choose **Start** when ready. Opening the board or crossing a worksite does not begin an encounter.

The Accepted board shows your current instructions and destination. Hover over a contract card or a disabled action to see its requirements; the quest tracker and Instructions retain the full preparation checklist and objective progress. A two-resource basket moves its destination to the second patch when the first is finished. A reagent order changes from gathering to mixing at The True Note. Surveys mark the resource nodes you still need to sample, and the quest tracker shows the sample count alongside any remaining gathering quota.

Check **Instructions** for preparation before starting: the usable recipe or spell, tools, ingredients, and other essentials are listed with their current readiness. Summoning assignments use your own companion **summoned inside the worksite**; a creature left on the public map does not accompany you into the private patrol.

The ordinary quest card controls still apply: collapse it to the ribbon, or use **More** for Instructions, Replay, Hide, and Untrack. Hiding or untracking guidance preserves the assignment.

## Work with your party

Field patrols are private to admitted party members. A party member can join at the marked public worksite using the Contracts board. Helpers keep their personal assignments.

If you accepted the same assignment with matching encounter terms, being present for a completed encounter advances its shared objective. A different assignment in the same discipline keeps its own objectives. Each player must also demonstrate the posted discipline. You do not need the killing blow. Summoning credit comes from your own summon; ordinary attacks by another player do not satisfy it.

Crew health is visible. Use **Heal Target** to recover fallen workers. You may leave an encounter and resume later. After defeat, an explicit retry restores the crew for another attempt while retaining completed checkpoints. It does not restore your health, ether, or supplies.

New Triage at the Watch assignments share the crew’s recovery goal across the party. One healer can restore the required health while the other casters defend with magic; each player still needs their own useful spell contribution. The Heal Target controls include living wounded crew while the recovery or your magic contribution remains unfinished, including after the attacks end. These are ordinary paid casts; fully healed crew stop appearing as patients. Older accepted Triage assignments keep their original personal healing objective.

## Report and rewards

Return to Batu when the posted objectives are complete. Each contract awards experience, gold, supplies, and **one service mark**, in addition to ordinary gameplay experience and loot.

Each assignment has a fixed difficulty. Experience is the multiplier below times your **base skill level, with a minimum level of one**. Melee applies the full multiplier separately to Attack and Defense, using each skill’s own base level. Accepting fixes those rewards even if you train further before reporting.

| Difficulty | Base gold | XP multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine | 500 | 150× |
| Standard | 1,500 | 400× |
| Demanding | 3,000 | 800× |
| Hard | 6,000 | 1,500× |
| Expert | 10,000 | 3,000× |

Some routes add gold, up to the 10,000-coin limit. Previously accepted assignments keep their original reward terms.

Each discipline also has a **bonus day once every three UTC days**, when new offers include an extra skill-supply parcel alongside those normal rewards:

| Discipline | Parcel examples |
| --- | --- |
| Melee | Bandages or Potions of Minor Healing |
| Magic | Life, Fire, or Water Essence |
| Ranging | A reusable Basic Quiver or Basic Bolt Case |
| Summoning | Bones, Reed, and Aether Salt |
| Harvesting | Bread or Cooked Meat |
| Alchemy | Full Life, Fire, or Water Essence ingredient batches |

Parcels rotate by day. Changing templates or routes, rerolling, or using the testing reset does not change that discipline’s parcel for the day. Already accepted assignments keep their rewards across day or level changes.

You keep harvested and mixed goods unless the posted assignment explicitly requires a handover. **A Shelf Ready for Sale** requires the listed ordinary essences when you report. Enriched results count toward mixing practice, but are never taken for the handover; mix enough ordinary stock to fill the order.

If that ordinary stock is missing, the card shows what to prepare instead of telling you to report immediately. Once the stock is ready, the destination returns to Batu.

The Apprentice’s Order can instead offer an optional donation of ordinary essences for extra gold. Its normal report remains available without donating. Hover over **Report + donate** to see which items it takes before choosing.

If your pack fills while gathering, store or unload the goods and return to the patch. The fresh gathering already recorded stays counted.

Exchange service marks with Batu for posted consumable supplies. Completing **five contracts in a UTC week**, in any disciplines, automatically awards **three extra marks**. There is no consecutive-day requirement.

## Daily reset

You may collect one completion per discipline per UTC day. The board displays the next reset. Unfinished accepted work survives the reset and disconnects, but still uses that discipline’s allowance on the day you report it.

One free replacement is available per UTC day before starting that discipline’s work. Use the posted replacement control at Batu; it replaces the unstarted posted offers rather than awarding anything.

These contracts are separate from the older Daily assignment and Ilka’s standing supply orders.

## Restart for testing

For development and quest testing, use `#reset dailies all` to restart your own daily assignments, or replace `all` with `melee`, `magic`, `ranging`, `summoning`, `harvesting`, or `alchemy`. Repeat the same command within 30 seconds after reading its confirmation. You must be alive, out of combat, and outside a private encounter.

The selected disciplines get fresh offers; their accepted progress, daily completion limit, and replacement cooldown are reset. Their old scenes and choices cannot be reused. Other assignments stay intact. Experience, gold, inventory, service marks, weekly completion progress, and already paid weekly bonuses are preserved. This testing command does not undo rewards you already received.
