BELLWATCH / 144 × 144 TILE CONCEPT

A refuge you can
always find.

Four short encounter pockets circle a warm central court. Gates open in sequence; unlocked return paths stay available. The waiting cart gives every fight a purpose.

One instruction. One live target. Map and viewport markers agree, and arriving always reveals the next action.

“You brought a boat home.
Help me bring these people through.”
— Ilyon

BLOCKING STUDY · WEST → BELL COURT
FRAME 012 min

The first bell

Find the place before the fight.

“North orchard. Find our lookout first.”

PLAY Walk to Ilyon, accept through dialogue, open Map and send #il in normal chat.

LEARN A public remaining count is not a local objective or personal kill count.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Quest accepted; correct approach reached; real map and command response.

Observe: Can the player find North and point out the refuge without being shown?

BLOCKING STUDY · BELL COURT
FRAME 023 min

Leave room to come home

Preparation changes the next encounter.

“A shield, a drink, and room for what you bring back.”

PLAY Store the bulky bundle, withdraw supplies and equip a real weapon and shield.

LEARN Equipment, healing supplies and spare carrying capacity all matter.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Actual transfers, equipped slots and sufficient room for the minimum loot.

Observe: Try full slots and equipment worn before the quest. Neither should block progress.

BLOCKING STUDY · NORTH · ORCHARD GATE
FRAME 033 min

Not every creature is the same

Watch, choose an approach, commit.

“Take it where you have room.”

PLAY Observe a red-named invader, click it to fight and collect its real drop.

LEARN Invaders wander and attack at reach; a non-red creature is not automatically safe.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Tagged invader defeated and its loot received, manually or through auto-gather.

Observe: Move the enemy away from its spawn. Its marker must follow it.

BLOCKING STUDY · EAST · RETURN LANE
FRAME 044 min

Coming back is part of fighting

Retreat while you still have a choice.

“Two now. Come back while you can still choose.”

PLAY Click clear ground while engaged, retry a failed flee, then use a healing potion.

LEARN Fleeing can fail and spends a combat round. Recovery is an active decision.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Successful server flee, refuge arrival, useful potion heal and encounter resolved.

Observe: Exercise a failed flee. Does the player understand the feedback and recover?

BLOCKING STUDY · EAST · MILL BREACH
FRAME 054 min

A quiet lane is not the end

A lull can hide the next wave.

“A wave is down. The breach is still feeding it.”

PLAY Clear one wave, react to reinforcements, resupply, then close this breach at its winch.

LEARN One-shot groups, further waves and respawns can produce different endings.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Both finite groups resolved and the real winch interaction accepted.

Observe: A zero count during the lull must not declare the route clear.

BLOCKING STUDY · SOUTH · STAGING LANE
FRAME 063 min

A shot is an invitation

Distance buys time, not invisibility.

“Distance buys you time. It does not make you unseen.”

PLAY Equip a bow and ammunition, fire at the sentry and respond to its approach.

LEARN Ranged aggression can trigger pursuit even when ordinary pursuit is disabled.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Server-resolved shot, reachable retaliation and a safely resolved encounter.

Observe: Test missed shots, missing arrows and a fresh character’s equipment requirements.

BLOCKING STUDY · SOUTH · MUSTER COURT
FRAME 072 min

Find the captain

Read the fight before entering it.

“Look at the weapons. Look at the size.”

PLAY Inspect the large named captain, its weapons and followers; choose the open lane.

LEARN Bosses anchor a group. The first boss need not wait for a kill quota.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Correct boss engaged through a reachable approach; understanding checked in play.

Observe: Can the player identify the boss using more than red color alone?

BLOCKING STUDY · SOUTH · BOSS ENCOUNTER
FRAME 085 min

The captain answers back

React to a fight that changes.

“More at its feet. Make room, then finish it.”

PLAY Fight through limited healing, respond to summoned adds and defeat the captain.

LEARN Heals and summons have finite budgets. This warband disperses when its boss falls.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Real heal and summon observed, boss defeated, survivors removed and combat cleared.

Observe: No endless healing wall, fake health animation or follower kill credit on dispersal.

BLOCKING STUDY · WEST · DEPARTURE ROAD
FRAME 095 min

The road without a voice

Use what you learned without a prompt.

“I will stay with them. You know what to look for.”

PLAY Assess a larger Great captain, prepare, fight, recover and check for surviving enemies.

LEARN Familiar bosses can vary in strength. This group does not disperse automatically.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: Departure group resolved, no pending wave and cart approach safe; strategy may vary.

Observe: Record extra help as assisted. A different successful strategy still counts.

BLOCKING STUDY · BELL COURT → FOUR GATES
FRAME 102 min

The second bell

Finish the rescue; understand its limits.

“You cleared a road, not the whole world.”

PLAY Collect loot, settle borrowed supplies, ring the bell, inspect Counters and return.

LEARN A cleared local road is not a cleared world. Private practice does not inflate public totals.

Completion evidence & playtest probe

Evidence: One-time reward, bell interaction and actual return-map transition.

Observe: With another invasion still active, Bellwatch must finish while #il stays truthful.

WHAT THIS MUST TEACH

Read the invasion, not just the next marker.

Threat recognition, movement and aggression, gear and capacity, retreat and healing, ranged retaliation, waves and respawns, boss strength and loadouts, limited healing, summoned adds, optional dispersal, loot and truthful public counters.

Gauntlets, territory raids and specialty events have distinct rules. Their admission, capture and reward systems do not become universal invasion rules in this story.

HOW WE WILL KNOW

A changed road. Less help.

Start with six new players at normal combat timing. Observe their decisions, then change the final encounter and remove mechanical prompts. Initial targets: five finish without facilitator control instructions; four complete the changed encounter independently.

Audit every checkpoint for reconnect, defeat, lost supplies, wrong markers, pending waves and duplicate rewards. A solo run does not prove multiplayer coordination.

ORGANIZER REHEARSAL / OPTIONAL

Stage it. Play it. Verify it.

On an isolated local server, an authorized invasion master practices the real Assistant: live maps, group duplication and building, composition, spawning, boss configuration, respawn windows, broadcasts and cleanup. A separate ordinary account plays the encounters.

BUILD BOUNDARY

Real actions. New story content.

Existing combat, inventory, flee, boss responses and bags stay authoritative. Bellwatch, its guide, checkpoints, refuge, winch, novice bosses and cart presentation are now playable in the native client. Private progress must not pretend to be public invasion credit.
