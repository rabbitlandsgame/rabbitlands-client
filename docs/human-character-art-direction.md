# High Human character art direction

The approved Human player studies define the High character head, neck and
hair style. Use their front, side and three-quarter views together, in both
equipped and underclothes studies. No rear study exists; record inferred rear
design separately. Rowan and Mira are the two quality-first study targets.

The user's direction on 2026-10-27 supersedes older Eternal Lands/Oldcraft
character style limits for these High surfaces. Preserve compatibility-sensitive
names and the Eloria lore; those identifiers do not impose a visual style.
Authored concept hair must retain its own material finish rather than receiving
the legacy matte/desaturation finish or source-hair reshaping intended for the
older models. Existing Low/Medium models and other assets keep their settings.

Match the reference's anatomy, eye openings, brow angle, lip shape, skin color
planes, freckles, curved hair clumps and silhouette. Keep the accepted 77-joint
rig, rest/binds, torso/hands, animation curves, clothing fit and weapon sockets.
Hair is static Head-bound geometry with pinned roots and controlled cosmetic
length/volume; no strand simulation, extra bones or per-frame CPU rebuilding.

Fit the lower neck from its anatomical neck boundary to the canonical body
rim. Preserve connected edge order when joining differently sampled rings.
Do not compress a shoulder-wide source patch into a neck: neither a smooth
shading pass nor matching edge positions proves that surface is valid. Inspect
unclothed side views as well as collars, and check closure, winding and genuine
self-intersections after removing coincident UV splits from the audit.

Comparisons must be actual game renders. Fit the portrait-only pose and camera
to the reference eye positions and apparent scale, retain the full crown and
neck, and inspect all three angles. A portrait pose override must never change
the shared gameplay animations. Do not retouch rendered models or substitute
generated artwork into review images. Texture-authoring studies and reference
paint bakes are assets, not evidence of final game appearance.

Pixel/byte identity with a painted reference is not a valid cross-view 3D
acceptance promise: silhouette, perspective, lighting and occlusion differ.
Report measured feature/framing residuals and remaining visible differences
honestly. Keep original references, numbered authoring stages, hashes and
unretouched captures in ignored local storage. Measure draw calls, mesh/map
memory, package estimates and actual viewport costs before recommending adoption.
