The Wraith's Three Trials

Try three possible futures before spending on the one you will keep.

Native gameplay proposal30–40 min4 gates · 10 framesHands-on learning
Design proposal — not implemented or human-playtested. Timings are targets. Each frame specifies real input, evidence, recovery and a changed application; required implementation work is identified below.
Echo Court / 112 × 112 TILE CONCEPT

One refuge.
Four useful journeys.

Guided courts introduce a mechanic, change a constraint, combine skills and then ask the player to solve a new problem.

Return paths stay available. Movement instructions become action instructions on arrival. Map and viewport markers refer to the same real target.

Frame budgets total 35 minutes; the broader session range allows for individual pacing. Optional experiments are separate.

Premise, access and map specification

At Echo Court, the Wraith shows three travelers caught at the same broken crossing: a porter unable to carry the supplies, a keeper unable to call help and a guard unable to hold a crowded lane. The player borrows a possible future for each trial, changes it through real character controls and sees the consequences before making choices on the permanent character.

The Wraith: “I can lend you a possibility. What you keep must be your own choice.”

Three small lanterns fill as the travelers reach the refuge. The Wraith's reset is openly a practice service, not a promise of free ordinary respecialization. The player never has to buy a real perk or spend a permanent pickpoint to finish.

Access and four-gate map

Offer when unspent pickpoints first become relevant, with a repeat invitation from the Wraith. Borrowed profiles have a declared budget of earned points and practice gold, calculated from the actual chosen attribute, nexus and perk costs. Keep the offered budget visible; do not hard-code a number before selecting catalog tiers. Restore all base values, spent points, perks, toggles, gear-derived effects, XP and buffs on exit.

Propose 112 × 112 tiles. Echo Court (56,56) holds the Wraith and three lanterns. North (56,87) is Weight Walk, East (87,56) the Keeper's Pen, South (56,25) the Guard's Court and West (25,56) the Unwritten Road.

GateCompositionPurpose
North · Weight WalkSupply stacks and a short safe carry routeAttributes and observable derived capacity
East · Keeper's PenSummon recipe and open spawn spaceNexus access versus skill progression
South · Guard's CourtSingle and multiple-opponent lanesPerk costs, conditions and tradeoffs
West · Unwritten RoadA different supply problem and threat arrangementChoose a build under a finite budget

Gates open in sequence but each trial can be replayed from the refuge. A displayed build card is a proposed summary of real server state, not an alternative character-allocation system.

Blocking diagram · Frame 01 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 013 MIN

What belongs to you

PictureThe player's real Statistics face a translucent practice ledger. Three unlit lanterns sit behind the Wraith.

“Look at the points you have. You will leave with those choices still yours.”

PlayOpen Statistics and Perks, inspect available pickpoints and one unaffordable next tier, then enter the separate practice profile. Compare the real and borrowed budget labels. Open a purchase confirmation and cancel it before any mutation.

LearnAvailable points, already spent points, gold and requirements are distinct; opening a confirmation does not spend anything.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceCorrect profile snapshot, actual confirmation cancellation and unchanged practice values after cancel. Merely reading a guide paragraph is not purchase literacy.

Recovery / observePlayers with no earned real points can still enter. Can the tester find the actual cost and cancel without believing they changed the permanent character?

Blocking diagram · Frame 02 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 024 MIN

A pack has a limit

PictureA porter waits beside supplies whose load is slightly above the borrowed character's capacity. A safe drop-off lies nearby.

“One journey or two. First see what the pack can hold.”

PlayAttempt to take the proposed order, read the capacity rejection, then inspect the directly purchased Carry attribute and carrying capacity. Spend one practice point on Carry through the real Statistics confirmation. The current model gives 20 capacity per Carry point. Take a quantity that now fits and deliver it to the porter.

LearnAttribute choices have measurable effects outside combat. Taking two trips remains valid ordinary play; this trial specifically demonstrates a capacity change once.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceGenuine rejected load, confirmed point spend, authoritative capacity increase and successful pickup within the new limit.

Recovery / observeTune the stack against the actual capacity so one Carry purchase makes it fit. Do not teach the retired Physique/Coordination cross-attribute formula; those names remain only for legacy-save migration. Never falsify the derived statistic to fit the scene.

Blocking diagram · Frame 03 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 034 MIN

Access is not expertise

PictureIn East Pen, the Stag row names Animal Nexus as its blocker. A second card shows Summoning as a separate skill.

“Permission to try is not practice at succeeding.”

PlayInspect the current Stag recipe with sufficient borrowed Summoning but insufficient Animal Nexus. Spend one practice nexus point through ordinary controls, then perform the real summon. Compare the stable nexus value with skill XP after the action.

LearnNexus unlocks eligibility; using a skill earns its XP. Spending on nexus does not grant arbitrary skill levels.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceOne genuine nexus restriction, accepted purchase and server-owned summon. Capture nexus and XP separately.

Recovery / observeA failed mix still demonstrates an attempt, but the spawn demonstration needs a successful retry. Supply only the outstanding allowance. Let players inspect a different nexus without making them buy all six.

Blocking diagram · Frame 04 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 043 MIN

Practice leaves a different mark

PictureA small safe workstation and sparring lane share a readable Statistics view. XP feedback rises beside the chosen activity.

“Do the work once. Watch which number answers.”

PlayChoose a short craft or combat task already familiar to the player. Observe its actual skill and Overall XP change, then inspect earned and available pickpoints. If a level threshold is demonstrated, author the practice XP just below that real threshold and disclose the starting state.

LearnSkill experience, Overall progress and available pickpoints have related but distinct roles; not every successful action earns a point immediately.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceServer XP event and correct subsequent Statistics. No invented level-up when the threshold was not crossed.

Recovery / observeA capped or unrewarded action must be replaced with a valid short task. In replay, place the XP farther from the threshold and see whether the tester expects an automatic point after every action.

Blocking diagram · Frame 05 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 054 MIN

Buy an effect you can use

PictureSouth Court offers a single attacker and a later pair. The perk catalog stays readable from the refuge.

“Read what changes, what it costs, and when it applies.”

PlayInspect a currently visible combat perk with a deterministic measurable modifier, such as the appropriate catalog entry for multi-combat or a weapon arrangement. Read tier cost, gold, conflicts and conditions. Purchase its first tier, equip a compatible kit and run the matching encounter.

LearnA perk's effect depends on its actual conditions; buying a named benefit does not improve every situation.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceConfirmed purchase, correct active condition and authoritative effective modifier during ordinary play. Do not infer a perk's strength from one lucky damage roll.

Recovery / observeSelect the exact offered perk/tier during implementation from the visible catalog; hidden legacy entries are not public recommendations. A mismatched weapon gets a clear reason and a safe kit change.

Blocking diagram · Frame 06 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 063 MIN

A higher tier has a price

PictureTwo possible next purchases sit beside the remaining budget. One would leave insufficient points for the other.

“The second choice uses what the first one leaves.”

PlayInspect the next purchasable tier and compare it with saving points for a different requirement. Attempt an unaffordable or conflicting purchase in practice, read the real rejection, then choose an affordable option or deliberately keep the points. Run a brief activity that uses the resulting build.

LearnTiers, conflicts and a finite budget create opportunity costs. Saving points is a legitimate choice.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceGenuine blocker and a valid budget state followed by useful play. No compulsory maximum-tier purchase.

Recovery / observeThe Wraith can restore the disclosed trial baseline. Distinguish this reset from ordinary removal stones. Ask the tester to show the first cost they would have to meet, not to memorize every tier price.

Blocking diagram · Frame 07 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 073 MIN

What is worn, what is owned

PictureA practice gear rack offers an item with a registered perk effect beside an otherwise comparable item. The owned-tier display remains visible.

“Some strength comes from the thing you wear. Take it off and look again.”

PlayEquip and remove a catalog-valid item that contributes a perk or modifier. Compare owned tier with effective effect, then restore the appropriate kit. If the selected perk is toggleable only at its maximum tier, inspect that restriction; actual toggling is an optional extension using a qualified borrowed profile.

LearnPurchased ownership, temporary enablement and gear-derived effects are different states. Unequipping an item is not a refund.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceExact gear instance change and expected effective modifier delta while purchased tiers and point budget remain unchanged.

Recovery / observeValidate a real native item/affix combination first. If no visible example exists, register the practice exemplar explicitly. Do not invent a universal perk-toggle button or imply every perk can be switched off.

Blocking diagram · Frame 08 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 083 MIN

Reconsider without guessing

PictureThe Wraith lays a clearly labeled practice removal stone beside the earlier purchase ledger.

“Outside this court, changing your mind has its own requirements.”

PlayInspect a registered removal item appropriate to a purchased practice attribute, nexus or perk. Use it through ordinary item handling, observe the actual refund/removal and inspect a dependent action that may now be blocked. Only then use the Wraith's disclosed practice reset to prepare the final trial.

LearnReal removal is item-specific and validated; a tutorial reset is not a public free respec service.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceReal item consumption and matching ownership/point delta. Refund quantity must come from the selected removal rule, not an assumed universal one-point refund.

Recovery / observeSupply one compatible practice stone and preserve its transaction ID. Wrong-item or minimum-attribute rejections must be safe. Verify the player does not confuse a toggle, unequip and removal.

Blocking diagram · Frame 09 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 096 MIN

Choose a future for the crossing

PictureWest Road combines a supply load, an optional summon route and a manageable guard encounter. Its arrangement differs from all three trials.

“These people need a way through. Spend only what your plan needs.”

PlayStart with a fresh declared practice budget. Inspect the task and select an attribute/nexus/perk combination, then execute the plan through actual pickup, summoning, crafting or combat. Change two variables between attempts: load threshold and threat arrangement. Provide at least two affordable validated routes.

LearnBuild around an intended activity and constraints instead of copying a prescribed allocation.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceBudget-valid real purchases and successful traveler/supply delivery. Accept spare points, multiple trips and alternate tactics; the player must apply at least one chosen change in the resulting action.

Recovery / observePreview costs before commitment and offer an explicit practice reset after a failed plan. Record why the tester chose a change and whether its actual effect solved the intended problem.

Blocking diagram · Frame 10 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 102 MIN

Leave the choice unwritten

PictureThe three lanterns glow. The practice ledger closes, leaving the real Statistics and unchanged unspent points.

“You have seen several roads. You do not owe me a choice today.”

PlayRestore the permanent profile, inspect one real next purchase and open then cancel its confirmation if the player wants to rehearse. Record a proposed next goal in the tutorial journal without spending. Leave or replay a trial.

LearnThe player owns the eventual commitment; borrowed progress did not purchase their real build.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceExact restoration of base values, earned/spent points, perk tiers/toggles, gear and XP; completion once. Any optional real purchase occurs outside tutorial requirements.

Recovery / observeHandle departure during an open confirmation by clearing the practice request. Later ask the player to find the cost and condition for their intended activity; a deliberate decision to save points passes.

Reflections take 5–7 minutes each. Coverage means understanding each category and trying representative changes, not buying every perk or nexus. Catalog prices and visibility are authoritative; archived perks are not offered simply because code still recognizes them.

Implementation dependencies and audit

New work includes Echo Court, three trial NPCs, profile budget snapshots, exact source/effective comparison display, practice-only reset, evidence and proposed journal goal note. Reuse real Statistics/Perks confirmation and server spend/removal paths. The active item catalog currently contains no removal stones even though removal handlers exist: core 08 requires a registered practice item with a handler-recognized name, native icon and normal item-use route. This content registration is a dependency, not an existing item the novice can already buy. The current character model has twelve directly purchased attributes; the old six-attribute system must not appear as the lesson's allocation model.

Audit no points, exact remaining budget, insufficient gold, attribute/nexus maximum, tier ceiling, conflicts, hidden perk, toggle below maximum tier, gear removal during an active effect, removal stone at minimum base value, item-instance replacement, two clicks on confirmation, reconnect after spend and early exit with a prompt open. Verify that restoring a practice build does not erase permanent guild/god state or a preexisting buff. Test all final-route budgets against actual costs.

Human playtest emphasis

Use the shared protocol. Target 5/6 distinguishing skill practice from nexus access through an action, and 4/6 solving the final task with a valid self-chosen allocation. At least 5/6 must show cancellation and correctly identify the remaining real budget after departure. Do not grade a specific build as correct when several routes work. A later task asks players to diagnose one real requirement and choose whether to spend, save or change the plan.

Source audit

  • Perks, point spending and removal, character model, statistics: costs, tiers, derived effects and ownership.
  • Native Statistics/Perks controls: actual confirmation, cancellation and catalog presentation.
  • World, recipes: real activity/XP, equipment and eligibility effects.
