The Unlit Shrine

Rehearse an oath, fulfill its terms and discover what is yours to choose.

Votive Crossing / 112 × 112 TILE CONCEPT

One refuge.
Four useful journeys.

Guided courts introduce a mechanic, change a constraint, combine skills and then ask the player to solve a new problem.

Return paths stay available. Movement instructions become action instructions on arrival. Map and viewport markers refer to the same real target.

Frame budgets total 35 minutes; the broader session range allows for individual pacing. Optional experiments are separate.

Premise, access and map specification

At Votive Crossing, the road's shrines have lost their lantern oil and their keepers have lost contact with the surrounding settlements. Archivist Sen asks the player to rehearse a pilgrimage using borrowed offerings and borrowed standing. As each delivery is completed, Sen lights a lamp and a keeper returns to the central court. The lamps are quest presentation; worship's actual effects remain character standing, skill XP and temporary blessings.

Sen: “An oath begins a relationship. Read its first page, not only its last reward.”

The core path uses Lucaa, whose actual offerings come from Storage and whose associated skill is Harvesting. Optional rehearsals cover other patrons and inventory-sourced offerings. Choosing Lucaa here is a teaching fixture, not a recommendation that every character should worship her.

Access and four-gate map

Offer on the first priest visit or after character-development practice. Participation is optional, including after completion. The permanent character may remain unaffiliated. Borrow isolated god ranks, inventory, Storage, skills, XP, buffs and gold. Record and restore any real worship and active blessings exactly; do not renounce a real god to enter.

Propose 112 × 112 tiles. Lamp Court (56,56) contains Sen and the private Storage keeper. North (56,87) is the Hall of Names, East (87,56) the Offering Garden, South (56,25) the Blessing Walk and West (25,56) the Pilgrim Road.

Lucaa's current first five offerings are Blue Lupine ×500, Red Rose ×500, Coal ×1000, Sulfur ×1000 and Iron Ore ×1000, all from Storage. Blue Lupine and Red Rose are referenced by the god data but absent from the active item/resource catalog. Register both items, native representations and real practice harvest definitions as new content before this path is playable; the storyboard does not claim those flowers can already be gathered. The tutorial lends the exact offering quantities minus a tiny demonstrable gathering shortfall. It never asks a novice to grind thousands of resources. Public offering quantities need not change.

Blocking diagram · Frame 01 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 013 MIN

Twelve names, one first question

PictureTwelve modest shrine symbols encircle a neutral archive table. Only two patron descriptions are expanded at once.

“Which work matters to you? Start there.”

PlayInspect Lucaa's actual priest dialogue and one contrasting patron's skill, enemies, starting standing and offering. Choose to enter Sen's borrowed pilgrimage, or pause and leave without joining anyone. The proposed comparison board summarizes current server definitions rather than creating a separate worship menu.

LearnPatrons support different skills and have incompatible relationships. Worship is optional.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceOrientation and explicit entry into a separate practice state. No permanent affiliation changes or automatic joining on dialogue open.

Recovery / observeExisting worshippers inspect their own restored status later. Keep the opt-out as visible as the practice invitation. Can the tester find the starting consequence, not just the maximum bonus?

Blocking diagram · Frame 02 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 023 MIN

The first rank is below zero

PictureLucaa's priest shows rank -2 beside a quiet harvest plot. The borrowed-status label stays visible.

“You begin with something to prove. Look at the work before you make the offering.”

PlayJoin Lucaa through ordinary priest dialogue in the practice profile. Read the actual rank -2 and -8% Harvesting XP state, then perform one XP-eligible harvest and inspect its server result. Compare it with a recorded equal-condition unaffiliated baseline from the same practice setup.

LearnJoining is not an immediate positive XP bonus. Current worship modifies the patron's skill by four percentage points per rank.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceReal join mutation and an eligible skill-XP event under negative standing. Do not demand an exact visible difference when integer rounding hides it.

Recovery / observeUse a resource whose base XP makes the difference readable and keep the practice hourly harvest XP allowance available. A capped harvest cannot demonstrate the intended effect.

Blocking diagram · Frame 03 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 034 MIN

An offering comes from somewhere

PictureA nearly complete Blue Lupine offering sits in Storage; a few flowers are in the player's pack beside the priest.

“The request says from Storage. Carrying it here is not the same thing.”

PlayAttempt the actual offering with a small storage shortfall despite sufficient combined pack-and-store stock. Read the missing amount, deposit the final flowers through real Storage, return to the priest and submit the offering. Rank rises from -2 to -1 and the exact storage quantity is consumed.

LearnOffering source and quantity are explicit requirements; inventory and Storage do not combine automatically.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceGenuine rejection without partial consumption, real deposit, one successful offering and correct rank/stock delta.

Recovery / observeSupplies remain nearby and the objective tracks the source shortfall. A double-click must not consume the next offering unintentionally. Can the player correct the source rather than collect another entire batch?

Blocking diagram · Frame 04 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 044 MIN

Finish the work behind the gift

PictureEast's rose bed has a short walk back to the store. Sen carries a lantern to a waiting keeper after the offering.

“A few roses are still missing. Bring those, and the request is complete.”

PlayHarvest a tiny missing portion of the next Red Rose offering from actual placed resources, deposit it with the lent remainder and submit through the priest. Observe rank -1 become 0, then review the next request. The lantern lights after the real offering event.

LearnOfferings are sequential, consume exact requested materials and change standing. The next request may require a different material and scale.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceActual harvest receipts, exact completed offering and next-request identity. No demand to collect all 500 roses.

Recovery / observeA misplaced or depleted resource must get a reachable replacement tied to the same objective identity. Existing practice stock counts. Observe whether the player checks the new request instead of repeating the old one.

Blocking diagram · Frame 05 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 053 MIN

Standing changes the work

PictureThe same harvest plot is seen at rank 0, then after the supplied Coal offering raises standing to +1.

“Now repeat the work. See which reward changed.”

PlayPerform the same eligible harvest at rank 0, submit the actual Coal offering from Storage, then repeat at rank +1. Inspect Harvesting XP and the active patron modifier. Keep other XP multipliers, resource type and practice skill constant.

LearnRank affects the associated skill's XP multiplier; it is not a flat increase to every skill or a permanent skill-level purchase.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceReal offering transition plus matched ordinary XP events and correct effective multiplier. A guide may explain rounding without changing the awarded XP.

Recovery / observeIf a special-day or area bonus is active, display it or use a neutral practice configuration. Do not falsely attribute the entire XP result to worship. Ask the tester to show the affected skill.

Blocking diagram · Frame 06 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 063 MIN

Some oaths cannot share a roof

PictureA nearby Unolas alcove is reachable from the same refuge, with both patrons' names visible.

“Before another oath, read who it asks you to leave behind.”

PlayWhile worshipping Lucaa in practice, inspect and attempt to join Unolas through ordinary dialogue. Read the real incompatibility rejection; retain Lucaa's rank and offerings unchanged. Inspect another nonconflicting patron without requiring a second commitment.

LearnConflicts are enforced in either direction. The current system permits at most three simultaneous gods, subject to those conflicts.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceActual rejected join with unchanged state. A red warning alone is not the whole lesson; the player must find why this pair conflicts.

Recovery / observeDo not renounce automatically to make the next scene work. The three-god cap is explored in an optional separate profile. Existing permanent affiliations remain isolated throughout.

Blocking diagram · Frame 07 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 074 MIN

A blessing is borrowed strength

PictureTwo prepared offering crates sit by Storage. A paid blessing option becomes available after the keeper accepts them.

“Standing opens this service. The blessing still has its own cost and duration.”

PlaySubmit the supplied Sulfur and Iron Ore offerings through actual dialogue to reach Lucaa rank 3. Inspect the newly available blessing, withdraw the required practice gold and purchase it. Current Lucaa data grants +10 temporary Harvesting levels for 25 gold at rank 3; values must be drawn from her live definition.

LearnStanding/XP multiplier, paid blessing and base skill are different. Other gods have different thresholds, amounts and prices.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceTwo real rank increments, one paid blessing, exact coin deduction and temporary-skill update. No permanent level or extra reward granted by the guide.

Recovery / observeEarlier clicking cannot skip offering order. Insufficient gold offers withdrawal rather than a free simulated blessing. Verify the player can still locate the unchanged base skill.

Blocking diagram · Frame 08 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 083 MIN

Watch the temporary part fade

PictureA safe plot and buff/stat display stay in view as one normal game-minute tick passes.

“Use the help while it lasts. Your training remains when it goes.”

PlayHarvest with the blessing active, inspect base versus temporary skill and observe one ordinary temporary-skill decay tick. Check a recipe/resource requirement whose effective behavior is actually influenced by that skill, without promising that every threshold accepts temporary levels. Repeat blessing purchase only in an optional test of its real refresh behavior.

LearnA blessing is temporary. A second purchase does not imply unlimited additive stacking.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceReal affected activity and temporary modifier decay while base skill/standing remain correct. Do not wait for the entire blessing to expire.

Recovery / observeLogout handles remaining temporary effects through the normal practice lifecycle. Record whether the player mistakes the temporary number for permanent progress or treats one decay tick as losing worship rank.

Blocking diagram · Frame 09 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 096 MIN

A pilgrim's own decision

PictureWest offers a new patron request in a clearly reset practice vignette, plus a neutral route for a traveler who chooses no oath.

“Read the request, its source, and the relationship. Then choose what to do.”

PlayEnter a disclosed fresh practice checkpoint with a catalog-valid nonconflicting patron and a small shortfall in an inventory-sourced offering. Inspect, obtain the missing materials and complete it, or deliberately decline that patron after demonstrating the blocker and use the neutral delivery route. Then rehearse renouncing and rejoining a practiced patron in the borrowed profile to observe the reset to -2.

LearnApply source, affordability and compatibility to a changed case; renouncing discards standing and rejoining starts over.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceA correct offering or explicit informed alternative, plus actual practice renounce/rejoin state. No real affiliation is changed.

Recovery / observeSelect the exact inventory offering from current definitions and lend its bulk. Avoid scripted free retention of the old rank. The objective does not name the priest option to click.

Blocking diagram · Frame 10 · Proposed gameplay
FRAME 102 MIN

Leave with your own beliefs

PictureThe shrine lamps are lit, but the central exit remains neutral. Sen returns the player's original worship ledger.

“You can leave without an oath. Knowing the terms was the lesson.”

PlayRestore the permanent character, inspect actual gods, standing and any preexisting blessing. Choose to visit a real priest for information or depart. If the player wants to join publicly, that is a separate voluntary ordinary action, never a tutorial completion requirement.

LearnPractice offerings, ranks, temporary levels and gold do not leave the shrine. Worship remains a character choice.

Evidence, recovery & observation

EvidenceExact original affiliations, inventory/storage, skill/XP and buff state restored; no exported practice offering value; journal completion once.

Recovery / observeExit during an offering must settle exactly once in practice. On the later transfer task ask the player to find a real starting rank and next offering source without purchasing or renouncing anything.

The shared god definition contains Aluwen extra-benefit text, but the inspected generic priest response path does not expose its free-heal/armor service options. Those services are not mandatory lessons until actual handlers, menus and item availability are verified or implemented. Do not teach metadata as a usable feature. Likewise, do not assert a Godless restriction unless the active join path enforces it.

Implementation dependencies and audit

New work includes Votive map/practice priest instances, a neutral comparison board, profile-safe god service routing, generated offering allowances, exact offering evidence and visible temporary/base comparisons. Priest names and service mappings must use registered identities; newly named decorative clergy cannot silently invoke another priest's service. Register the two missing core flowers and their harvest/tool rules. Several other patrons also reference absent legacy items; validate and register every optional offering's item/instance/model before enabling its rehearsal. Jayden's first five potion offerings are present in the current catalog and provide a lower-content-cost alternative if the core is reskinned around potion production. That is an alternative design, not a silent runtime substitution for Lucaa.

Audit storage versus inventory sources, mixed offerings, insufficient items/gold, repeated response packets, rank limits, conflict in either direction, three-god cap, already worshipping, renounce/rejoin, offering of tracked equipment and instance-ledger consistency, active blessing on entry/exit, research/harvest XP caps, special-day bonuses and disconnect during an offering. The current offering helper mutates inventory/storage quantities directly; validate exact tracked-item ownership in the surrounding service before teaching equipment offerings.

Human playtest emphasis

Target 5/6 demonstrating the correct offering source and recognizing negative initial standing, and 4/6 resolving the changed request without mechanical hints. At least 5/6 must distinguish an XP multiplier, temporary blessing and permanent skill through actual Statistics/activity evidence. A deliberate choice to remain unaffiliated passes. The later real-character task is informational and cannot require sacrificing belongings.

Source audit

  • God definitions and transitions: twelve patrons, rank -2 through 5, offerings, conflicts, cap and blessing parameters.
  • Priest and XP runtime: actual dialogue responses, generic services, XP modifiers and temporary skill lifecycle.
  • Native Statistics/dialogue, item instances: display and exact ownership requirements.
