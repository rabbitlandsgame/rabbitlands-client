# The Unlit Shrine

**Gods and worship storyboard · Proposal · Solo · 30–40 minutes · Ten core frames**

[Illustrated storyboard](storyboard.html) · [Collection](../README.md) · [Shared contract](../design-contract.md)

At **Votive Crossing**, the road's shrines have lost their lantern oil and their keepers have lost contact with the surrounding settlements. **Archivist Sen** asks the player to rehearse a pilgrimage using borrowed offerings and borrowed standing. As each delivery is completed, Sen lights a lamp and a keeper returns to the central court. The lamps are quest presentation; worship's actual effects remain character standing, skill XP and temporary blessings.

**Sen:** “An oath begins a relationship. Read its first page, not only its last reward.”

The core path uses **Lucaa**, whose actual offerings come from Storage and whose associated skill is Harvesting. Optional rehearsals cover other patrons and inventory-sourced offerings. Choosing Lucaa here is a teaching fixture, not a recommendation that every character should worship her.

## Access and four-gate map

Offer on the first priest visit or after character-development practice. Participation is optional, including after completion. The permanent character may remain unaffiliated. Borrow isolated god ranks, inventory, Storage, skills, XP, buffs and gold. Record and restore any real worship and active blessings exactly; do not renounce a real god to enter.

Propose **112 × 112 tiles**. Lamp Court (56,56) contains Sen and the private Storage keeper. North (56,87) is the Hall of Names, East (87,56) the Offering Garden, South (56,25) the Blessing Walk and West (25,56) the Pilgrim Road.

| Gate | Composition | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North · Hall of Names | Priest alcoves and a neutral departure door | Compare patrons and understand commitment |
| East · Offering Garden | Storage beside small real harvest plots | Sources, quantities, rank and XP |
| South · Blessing Walk | Quiet practice resource and visible buff display | Paid temporary skill effects |
| West · Pilgrim Road | A changed offering situation and free exit | Independent preparation and voluntary handoff |

Lucaa's current first five offerings are Blue Lupine ×500, Red Rose ×500, Coal ×1000, Sulfur ×1000 and Iron Ore ×1000, all from Storage. **Blue Lupine and Red Rose are referenced by the god data but absent from the active item/resource catalog.** Register both items, native representations and real practice harvest definitions as new content before this path is playable; the storyboard does not claim those flowers can already be gathered. The tutorial lends the exact offering quantities minus a tiny demonstrable gathering shortfall. It never asks a novice to grind thousands of resources. Public offering quantities need not change.

## Ten storyboard frames

### 01 · Twelve names, one first question — 3 minutes

**Picture:** Twelve modest shrine symbols encircle a neutral archive table. Only two patron descriptions are expanded at once.

**Voice:** “Which work matters to you? Start there.”

**Play:** Inspect Lucaa's actual priest dialogue and one contrasting patron's skill, enemies, starting standing and offering. Choose to enter Sen's borrowed pilgrimage, or pause and leave without joining anyone. The proposed comparison board summarizes current server definitions rather than creating a separate worship menu.

**Learn:** Patrons support different skills and have incompatible relationships. Worship is optional.

**Evidence:** Orientation and explicit entry into a separate practice state. No permanent affiliation changes or automatic joining on dialogue open.

**Recovery / observe:** Existing worshippers inspect their own restored status later. Keep the opt-out as visible as the practice invitation. Can the tester find the starting consequence, not just the maximum bonus?

### 02 · The first rank is below zero — 3 minutes

**Picture:** Lucaa's priest shows rank -2 beside a quiet harvest plot. The borrowed-status label stays visible.

**Voice:** “You begin with something to prove. Look at the work before you make the offering.”

**Play:** Join Lucaa through ordinary priest dialogue in the practice profile. Read the actual rank -2 and -8% Harvesting XP state, then perform one XP-eligible harvest and inspect its server result. Compare it with a recorded equal-condition unaffiliated baseline from the same practice setup.

**Learn:** Joining is not an immediate positive XP bonus. Current worship modifies the patron's skill by four percentage points per rank.

**Evidence:** Real join mutation and an eligible skill-XP event under negative standing. Do not demand an exact visible difference when integer rounding hides it.

**Recovery / observe:** Use a resource whose base XP makes the difference readable and keep the practice hourly harvest XP allowance available. A capped harvest cannot demonstrate the intended effect.

### 03 · An offering comes from somewhere — 4 minutes

**Picture:** A nearly complete Blue Lupine offering sits in Storage; a few flowers are in the player's pack beside the priest.

**Voice:** “The request says from Storage. Carrying it here is not the same thing.”

**Play:** Attempt the actual offering with a small storage shortfall despite sufficient combined pack-and-store stock. Read the missing amount, deposit the final flowers through real Storage, return to the priest and submit the offering. Rank rises from -2 to -1 and the exact storage quantity is consumed.

**Learn:** Offering source and quantity are explicit requirements; inventory and Storage do not combine automatically.

**Evidence:** Genuine rejection without partial consumption, real deposit, one successful offering and correct rank/stock delta.

**Recovery / observe:** Supplies remain nearby and the objective tracks the source shortfall. A double-click must not consume the next offering unintentionally. Can the player correct the source rather than collect another entire batch?

### 04 · Finish the work behind the gift — 4 minutes

**Picture:** East's rose bed has a short walk back to the store. Sen carries a lantern to a waiting keeper after the offering.

**Voice:** “A few roses are still missing. Bring those, and the request is complete.”

**Play:** Harvest a tiny missing portion of the next Red Rose offering from actual placed resources, deposit it with the lent remainder and submit through the priest. Observe rank -1 become 0, then review the next request. The lantern lights after the real offering event.

**Learn:** Offerings are sequential, consume exact requested materials and change standing. The next request may require a different material and scale.

**Evidence:** Actual harvest receipts, exact completed offering and next-request identity. No demand to collect all 500 roses.

**Recovery / observe:** A misplaced or depleted resource must get a reachable replacement tied to the same objective identity. Existing practice stock counts. Observe whether the player checks the new request instead of repeating the old one.

### 05 · Standing changes the work — 3 minutes

**Picture:** The same harvest plot is seen at rank 0, then after the supplied Coal offering raises standing to +1.

**Voice:** “Now repeat the work. See which reward changed.”

**Play:** Perform the same eligible harvest at rank 0, submit the actual Coal offering from Storage, then repeat at rank +1. Inspect Harvesting XP and the active patron modifier. Keep other XP multipliers, resource type and practice skill constant.

**Learn:** Rank affects the associated skill's XP multiplier; it is not a flat increase to every skill or a permanent skill-level purchase.

**Evidence:** Real offering transition plus matched ordinary XP events and correct effective multiplier. A guide may explain rounding without changing the awarded XP.

**Recovery / observe:** If a special-day or area bonus is active, display it or use a neutral practice configuration. Do not falsely attribute the entire XP result to worship. Ask the tester to show the affected skill.

### 06 · Some oaths cannot share a roof — 3 minutes

**Picture:** A nearby Unolas alcove is reachable from the same refuge, with both patrons' names visible.

**Voice:** “Before another oath, read who it asks you to leave behind.”

**Play:** While worshipping Lucaa in practice, inspect and attempt to join Unolas through ordinary dialogue. Read the real incompatibility rejection; retain Lucaa's rank and offerings unchanged. Inspect another nonconflicting patron without requiring a second commitment.

**Learn:** Conflicts are enforced in either direction. The current system permits at most three simultaneous gods, subject to those conflicts.

**Evidence:** Actual rejected join with unchanged state. A red warning alone is not the whole lesson; the player must find why this pair conflicts.

**Recovery / observe:** Do not renounce automatically to make the next scene work. The three-god cap is explored in an optional separate profile. Existing permanent affiliations remain isolated throughout.

### 07 · A blessing is borrowed strength — 4 minutes

**Picture:** Two prepared offering crates sit by Storage. A paid blessing option becomes available after the keeper accepts them.

**Voice:** “Standing opens this service. The blessing still has its own cost and duration.”

**Play:** Submit the supplied Sulfur and Iron Ore offerings through actual dialogue to reach Lucaa rank 3. Inspect the newly available blessing, withdraw the required practice gold and purchase it. Current Lucaa data grants +10 temporary Harvesting levels for 25 gold at rank 3; values must be drawn from her live definition.

**Learn:** Standing/XP multiplier, paid blessing and base skill are different. Other gods have different thresholds, amounts and prices.

**Evidence:** Two real rank increments, one paid blessing, exact coin deduction and temporary-skill update. No permanent level or extra reward granted by the guide.

**Recovery / observe:** Earlier clicking cannot skip offering order. Insufficient gold offers withdrawal rather than a free simulated blessing. Verify the player can still locate the unchanged base skill.

### 08 · Watch the temporary part fade — 3 minutes

**Picture:** A safe plot and buff/stat display stay in view as one normal game-minute tick passes.

**Voice:** “Use the help while it lasts. Your training remains when it goes.”

**Play:** Harvest with the blessing active, inspect base versus temporary skill and observe one ordinary temporary-skill decay tick. Check a recipe/resource requirement whose effective behavior is actually influenced by that skill, without promising that every threshold accepts temporary levels. Repeat blessing purchase only in an optional test of its real refresh behavior.

**Learn:** A blessing is temporary. A second purchase does not imply unlimited additive stacking.

**Evidence:** Real affected activity and temporary modifier decay while base skill/standing remain correct. Do not wait for the entire blessing to expire.

**Recovery / observe:** Logout handles remaining temporary effects through the normal practice lifecycle. Record whether the player mistakes the temporary number for permanent progress or treats one decay tick as losing worship rank.

### 09 · A pilgrim's own decision — 6 minutes

**Picture:** West offers a new patron request in a clearly reset practice vignette, plus a neutral route for a traveler who chooses no oath.

**Voice:** “Read the request, its source, and the relationship. Then choose what to do.”

**Play:** Enter a disclosed fresh practice checkpoint with a catalog-valid nonconflicting patron and a small shortfall in an inventory-sourced offering. Inspect, obtain the missing materials and complete it, or deliberately decline that patron after demonstrating the blocker and use the neutral delivery route. Then rehearse renouncing and rejoining a practiced patron in the borrowed profile to observe the reset to -2.

**Learn:** Apply source, affordability and compatibility to a changed case; renouncing discards standing and rejoining starts over.

**Evidence:** A correct offering or explicit informed alternative, plus actual practice renounce/rejoin state. No real affiliation is changed.

**Recovery / observe:** Select the exact inventory offering from current definitions and lend its bulk. Avoid scripted free retention of the old rank. The objective does not name the priest option to click.

### 10 · Leave with your own beliefs — 2 minutes

**Picture:** The shrine lamps are lit, but the central exit remains neutral. Sen returns the player's original worship ledger.

**Voice:** “You can leave without an oath. Knowing the terms was the lesson.”

**Play:** Restore the permanent character, inspect actual gods, standing and any preexisting blessing. Choose to visit a real priest for information or depart. If the player wants to join publicly, that is a separate voluntary ordinary action, never a tutorial completion requirement.

**Learn:** Practice offerings, ranks, temporary levels and gold do not leave the shrine. Worship remains a character choice.

**Evidence:** Exact original affiliations, inventory/storage, skill/XP and buff state restored; no exported practice offering value; journal completion once.

**Recovery / observe:** Exit during an offering must settle exactly once in practice. On the later transfer task ask the player to find a real starting rank and next offering source without purchasing or renouncing anything.

The shared god definition contains Aluwen extra-benefit text, but the inspected generic priest response path does not expose its free-heal/armor service options. Those services are not mandatory lessons until actual handlers, menus and item availability are verified or implemented. Do not teach metadata as a usable feature. Likewise, do not assert a Godless restriction unless the active join path enforces it.

## Implementation dependencies and audit

New work includes Votive map/practice priest instances, a neutral comparison board, profile-safe god service routing, generated offering allowances, exact offering evidence and visible temporary/base comparisons. Priest names and service mappings must use registered identities; newly named decorative clergy cannot silently invoke another priest's service. Register the two missing core flowers and their harvest/tool rules. Several other patrons also reference absent legacy items; validate and register every optional offering's item/instance/model before enabling its rehearsal. Jayden's first five potion offerings are present in the current catalog and provide a lower-content-cost alternative if the core is reskinned around potion production. That is an alternative design, not a silent runtime substitution for Lucaa.

Audit storage versus inventory sources, mixed offerings, insufficient items/gold, repeated response packets, rank limits, conflict in either direction, three-god cap, already worshipping, renounce/rejoin, offering of tracked equipment and instance-ledger consistency, active blessing on entry/exit, research/harvest XP caps, special-day bonuses and disconnect during an offering. The current offering helper mutates inventory/storage quantities directly; validate exact tracked-item ownership in the surrounding service before teaching equipment offerings.

## Human playtest emphasis

Target 5/6 demonstrating the correct offering source and recognizing negative initial standing, and 4/6 resolving the changed request without mechanical hints. At least 5/6 must distinguish an XP multiplier, temporary blessing and permanent skill through actual Statistics/activity evidence. A deliberate choice to remain unaffiliated passes. The later real-character task is informational and cannot require sacrificing belongings.
