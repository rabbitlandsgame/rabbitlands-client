# Enchanting equipment in Rabbit Lands

Open **Inventory → ••• → Enchant equipment…**, click **Enchant** on an equipment
inspection card, or use **Mixing → ••• → Enchant equipment…**. These controls
appear when the connected server supports enchanting.

Put the item in your backpack and unequip it first. Choose the individual copy,
pick an operation and materials, then click **Review enchantment** (or the
operation's Review button). The server checks the selection and opens a
confirmation with the exact cost. Nothing is spent until you confirm.
Your item keeps its identity, condition,
quality and creator. Blue names support up to two effects; yellow names support
up to four.

The copy list shows backpack slots and effect counts, so identical items stay
distinct. Pick an essence theme and quantity for plain equipment; **More options**
lets you combine themes. Current odds and total costs stay beside the main action.
Use the **Break Down**, **Refine** and **Enchant** tabs to move between windows.

| What you want | What to use |
| --- | --- |
| Enchant plain equipment | Choose 1–5 enriched essences. With one, the chances are 70% one effect, 25% two, 4% three and 1% four. More essences improve the effect-count odds. |
| Promote blue equipment | A blue item with two effects plus 10 Enriched Magic and 10 Enriched Life becomes yellow with a third effect, keeping both original rolls. |
| Add effects | Enriched Life fills available slots without changing name color. The fourth yellow effect costs extra Life. |
| Remove an effect | One Enriched Death removes a random effect. Three lets you choose. Removing the last effect makes the item plain. |
| Reroll numbers | Enriched Water keeps effect types and rerolls their values. More Water keeps the best of more fresh rolls; values can still decrease. |
| Reroll effect types | Enriched Magic keeps color and count. One rerolls all effects; three can preserve one chosen effect, five can preserve two. |

Optional enriched Fire, Frost, Earth or Wind catalysts favor their themes when
adding, promoting or rerolling effects. They do not guarantee a particular effect
or raise its possible values. The cost preview is authoritative for your server.
Life additions and Magic effect rerolls also favor their own essence themes.

Ordinary equipment crafting can also produce blue or yellow copies. Tutorial
crafting stays plain. If a recipe consumes equipment, **Mix Now** asks you to
select the exact copies for one attempt. Check their effects before confirming;
automatic queues cannot choose copies for you.

In gauntlets and the Serpent Deep raid, each equipment copy dropped by a
miniboss or final boss independently rolls its name color and effects:

| Enemy | Plain | Blue (1–2 effects) | Yellow (3–4 effects) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Miniboss | 60% | 30% | 10% |
| Final boss | 25% | 50% | 25% |

These chances apply after the enemy's normal item drop roll; they do not
guarantee equipment. Chests, caches and completion-choice rewards retain their
existing rules. Each enchanted copy appears separately in party loot, with a
colored name, effect icons and descriptions. Click its icon to inspect it before
allocating it. The leader assigns these copies individually; their saved effects
follow them through storage delivery and party exchange sales or withdrawals.

Unwanted enchanted equipment can be reclaimed through **Break Down & Refine…**.
**Break Down** shows the chosen copy's effects and exact returns, then destroys
that copy for Enchanting Shards and normal essences after confirmation.
**Refine** lists the essence recipes and shows required and owned materials.
Choose a quantity, then use **Refine** to review the shards, normal essences and
gold fee for the guaranteed enriched-essence result.
