# Reagent pouch

Spell reagents, anchors and Attunement Charges are held automatically in a
weightless pouch. They use no backpack slots. Existing carried quantities stay
intact and free their former slots when the character loads. Supplies already
in storage remain there until withdrawn.

Open **Inventory → Pouch** to see your supplies. Select a supply to inspect it,
deposit a chosen quantity while storage is open, or drop a chosen quantity.
The trade and storage item lists also include pouch supplies. Crafting and
looting put eligible materials directly into the pouch. Each type supports
reserves up to 4,294,967,295 units.

Spell details show **Casts now**, limited by current ether and supplies, and
**Supplies**, the number of casts supported by materials at the selected power.
These counts update when power, statistics or inventory changes. They use the
current quote, without predicting regeneration or future level-ups.

Foci remain ordinary inventory items. Their existing school matching and
Attunement Charge substitution are unchanged. Remaining casts include usable
ordinary anchors after focus charges run out. Ether and reagent costs are
unchanged; the pouch only removes material weight and backpack-slot pressure.

Pouch materials remain ordinary owned items for crafting, trading, dropping,
storage and the existing death rules. The pouch does not duplicate a balance,
grant free materials or automatically spend gold to replenish supplies.
