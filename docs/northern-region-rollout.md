# Northern landscapes

The second group extends Amberwood and Whitehorn with three 384 m regions.
Shorten empty travel space while retaining building, bridge and combat dimensions.
Their service centres and required entrances stay recognizable.

| Region | Composition | Travel and habitation |
|---|---|---|
| Grey Moors | Low peat ground, burial ridges, protected barrow crowns and quiet grazing | A sheltered working refuge, peat drying yards, broad bank-to-bank bridges, crypt approaches; meadow gives way to wet heather at Amberwood |
| Mirrorhold | An inhabited lower basin below steep civic terraces and the Orrery | Houses face connected lanes and smaller working yards; the quay, cargo route and higher civic road have separate grades; two distinct northern cols |
| Amethyst Barrens | A dry working basin below crystal seams and an exposed northern massif | Full-size outpost dwellings around the assay/cook/storage court; restrained gravel paths, scattered lee vegetation, a winding southern ascent and connected cargo jetty |

Broad landforms establish the region. Grade practical roads through them, place
major buildings and crossings, then scatter vegetation by habitat and human use.
Protect quiet ground. Revisit foundation heights and material boundaries from
the gameplay camera after compaction; a walkability mask alone cannot reveal an
ugly ledge, repeated checker paving or an obscured doorway.

Reapply public road grades after forming adjacent yards. Bridge approaches
must follow surveyed road heights rather than the eroded channel beneath them,
and their beds must leave the actual deck exposed. Remove obsolete buried
structures and cut proper road openings through retaining walls. Check the
server's real path lengths between nearby services: connectivity alone accepted
a detour of more than 500 metres at Mirrorhold's bridge turn.

The shared border toolkit defines continuity, not a continental visual template.
Use the same road-space coordinates and reciprocal frames while choosing the
ground, shoulders and vegetation appropriate to both neighbours. A marsh road
does not acquire snow just because an alpine road used the same toolkit.

These are proposed extents, subject to service and route checks. Preserve each
region's required connections, interiors, secrets and lore. Complete one linked
group at a time, including the receiving approaches in already finished maps.
Each group needs matched gameplay captures, revised minimaps, single-arrival
connectivity checks, live road/door walks and a shared client/server publication.
