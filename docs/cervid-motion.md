# Deer and elk motion

The six approved sets are Lantern Stag, Elderwood Stag, Autumn Antler Stag, Autumn Crown Stag, Crown Antler Stag and Frosthorn Elk. Their installed exports retain the reviewed native Walk (1.12 s), Jog/trot (0.66 s), antler-led Sword_Attack (1.60 s) and supported side-collapse Death_A (2.60 s), plus idle, fighting-idle and hit. The final attack and death poses hold from 1.45 s and 2.05 s respectively.

Each rig uses its own physical leg chains, coherent hoof surfaces and mirrored contact lanes. Body-relative foreleg bend planes are checked separately in the collapse. Compact crowned stags use feasible free-leg paths; Crown Antler has a longer brace and gentler fall inside the same clip durations. Elderwood chest foliage and Autumn Antler collar tufts have coherent torso/neck ownership, while nearby upper-leg fringe remains attached to the legs. Geometry, textures, inverse binds and rest poses are preserved. Original non-bone wrapper translations are baked into physical roots where required, preserving inherited physical world poses.

Dedicated maps under `data/animations/cervid/` keep existing server commands and play walk/run at authored speed. Measured strides use the actor's existing registry import scale and runtime actor scaling. Scales, facing and attachment mappings are unchanged. Tracked import policies retain the qualified 3072 FPS bake cadence with animation optimization/compression disabled.

`docs/qa/cervid-motion/actors.json` records hashes, native chains, sole probes and timing. Independent physical bone and skin witnesses across all seven slots are in `tests/fixtures/cervid_motion_worlds.json`, including repaired collar roots and tips. Full-resolution inputs, fitting stages, prepared skins and normal-speed six-angle review media stay in the ignored authoring workspace.

Run `python eloria-assets/tools/test_cervid_motion.py` from the repository root with NumPy and SciPy. After importing the project, run `tests/test_cervid_motion.gd`. The native check covers all 42 slots and 24 authored actions, surface floor/contact, serial lengths, loops and holds. The client component check uses the current scene cache, importer and action resolver, with independent off-grid bone/skin witnesses, action switching, idle restoration and RESET. These checks do not establish a full client/server play session or crowded rendering performance.
