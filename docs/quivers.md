# Quivers

Equip a bow and a quiver to select your shot type. Shooting never consumes the
quiver or its arrows. A quiver occupies the ammunition equipment slot and appears
on the character's back independently of shields and capes.

| Quiver | Shot effect |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | Ordinary physical damage |
| Hunter's | +3 raw ranging damage |
| Bodkin | -2 raw ranging damage; 25% chance to bypass armor |
| Farshot | 25% less damage loss over distance |
| Skirmisher's | 10 percentage points of relief from each melee ranging penalty |
| Ember | Converts 30% of raw shot damage to heat |
| Frost | Converts 30% of raw shot damage to cold |
| Arcane | Converts 30% of raw shot damage to magic |

Elemental conversions preserve the total raw damage and apply the corresponding
elemental protection. Armor and missile protection apply to the physical portion.
Farshot reduces distance loss after Perception; it does not extend maximum range.

Maximum range remains `6 + floor(Ranging / 10)` tiles. Normal legal shots keep
their 80% accuracy floor before the penalties for being engaged in melee.
Clicking a creature beyond that range makes your character approach until it is
within bow range, then begin shooting. Walking elsewhere or choosing another
target interrupts the approach.

New Rangers start with one equipped Basic Quiver. Basic Quivers can be crafted;
the quiver range is available from the existing bow suppliers. Older saved arrows
convert to the closest equivalent quiver when the updated server opens the save:
ordinary/training to Basic, Pking to Bodkin, fire to Ember, ice to Frost and magic
to Arcane. Saved quantities are preserved.

All eight designs have separate male and female fits for each of the eight player
races. Their cases and seated arrows follow the upper spine, while their straps
deform with the torso.
Basic Quiver's chest strap and buckle lie flat against the wearer. Thinner cases
use an authored clearance fit while a cape is equipped and return to their
normal back fit when the cape is removed. The Basic case keeps its accepted
position, and Skirmisher keeps its smaller case size.

Inventory icons use painted illustrations of the approved designs, with the
same dark teal background and 50-pixel cells as other equipment.
