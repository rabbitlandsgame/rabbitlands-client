# Insectivore motion

The approved Hedgehog, Shrew and Giant Mole sets use Walk (0.70 s), Jog/scurry (0.36 s), Sword_Attack/forward bite and body jab (1.60 s) and Death_A/supported collapse (2.60 s), plus inherited idle, fighting idle and hit. Attack and death hold from 1.45 s and 2.05 s.

Hedgehog uses physical detached leg guides and coherent quills, torso and face skin. Shrew has fitted shoulder/hip and elbow guides, complete forearm and paw fields, and protected face, ears, whiskers and torso. Giant Mole uses a declared rigid body-forward reframe, its actual neck-base head control, complete digging-hand/finger-helper and hind-heel ownership, native forearm reach and supported collapse. Both tails retain centered horizontal guides. Topology, UVs, embedded images and node hierarchy are preserved; intended geometry, rest/bind and skin changes are explicit in `docs/qa/insectivore-motion/preservation.json`.

Dedicated maps under `data/animations/insectivore/` retain gameplay action names. Measured stride speeds use each actor's existing registry scale; facing and attachment declarations are unchanged. Actor-specific importer policies retain 3072 FPS with optimization/compression disabled. Independent off-grid bone and skin witnesses cover all 21 slots and actual eight-influence meshes. Dense selected-export qualification covers 113,202 native samples; inherited actions retain their source floor baseline.

Run `python eloria-assets/tools/test_insectivore_motion.py` with NumPy and SciPy. After import, run `tests/test_insectivore_motion.gd`. These check contact, floor clearance, serial lengths, loops, held endpoints, coherent facial/torso cores and Hedgehog quill rigidity. Current-client component playback also covers action switching, RESET and idle restoration. This establishes component playback rather than a full client/server session or crowd-performance benchmark. Full-resolution sources, intermediate stages and review media remain ignored.
