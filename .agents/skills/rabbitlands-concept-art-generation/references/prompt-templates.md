# Prompt templates

Replace bracketed fields with authoritative Eloria details. Keep prompts concrete and omit irrelevant clauses.

## Humanoid strict-front master

> STRICT DEAD-FRONT full-body modeling reference of [character/race/class], [identity details]. Chunky stylized fantasy proportions, broad readable silhouette, simplified planar forms, hand-painted matte materials, restrained surface detail, [palette and equipment]. Neutral relaxed A-pose, arms about 30 degrees from the torso, feet shoulder-width and level on one ground plane, hands and face visible, shoulders and hips square to camera, equal left/right exposure, orthographic-like eye-level camera. Single centered subject, complete body in frame, uniform light-gray studio background, flat even lighting. No three-quarter angle, body twist, contrapposto, foreshortening, action pose, scenery, platform, text, border, duplicate character, cropped anatomy, or photorealism.

## Humanoid three-quarter support view

> The exact same [character/race/class] as the approved strict-front master, preserving face, proportions, outfit construction, equipment placement, palette, and materials. Neutral relaxed A-pose from a restrained front three-quarter modeling view, limited perspective, complete silhouette and both sides readable, flat light-gray background and even lighting. This is a support view, not a redesign. No action, changed accessories, dramatic camera, scenery, text, or crop.

## Orthographic side or back support view

> The exact same approved [asset] in the exact same neutral pose and scale, shown from a strict [left side/right side/back] orthographic-like camera at the same height. Preserve silhouette, proportions, anatomy, equipment, color boundaries, and material placement exactly. Complete subject centered on the same uniform neutral background with flat lighting. No three-quarter drift, rotation between views, redesign, added detail, text, scenery, or crop.

## Creature for 3D

> Full-body concept art of [species/name], [exact anatomy and distinctive features]. [Body-plan] construction with exactly [appendage counts], clear joint and attachment roots, chunky broad forms, strong game-readable silhouette, hand-painted matte color blocks, restrained detail, [palette/material notes]. Neutral [standing/swimming/flying-rest] pose, appendages separated, head aligned with body, complete silhouette visible, front three-quarter view with limited perspective. One isolated creature on pure white or light-gray background. No scenery, platform, cast shadow, labels, extra limbs, extra heads, hidden feet, action pose, or photorealism.

## Equipment or prop for 3D

> Isolated concept art of [item], designed for [use/character]. Chunky stylized fantasy construction, clear primary silhouette, visible thickness, attachment points and openings, hand-painted matte [materials], [palette and motifs], restrained ornament. Front three-quarter product view, complete object centered on a flat neutral background with soft even lighting. No hand holding it, no character, no scenery, no pedestal, no text, no border, no extreme perspective, no floating disconnected pieces.

## Overworld or region

> Wide elevated 16:9 environment concept of [region], preserving [authoritative geography]. Clearly show [paths, landmark hierarchy, settlements, boundaries, elevation, biome transitions], with navigable spacing and readable major masses. Chunky painterly Eloria fantasy style, simplified shapes, warm storybook palette, matte hand-painted surfaces, restrained atmospheric depth. No UI, labels, border, minimap symbols, photorealism, or decorative clutter that obscures traversal.

## Interior

> Wide 16:9 concept of [interior], a playable space with [entrance, exit, traversal path, focal landmark, encounter area, vertical change]. Chunky hand-painted Eloria fantasy style, broad readable architecture, simplified materials, warm controlled lighting, clear navigation and gameplay scale. No UI, labels, border, fisheye lens, impossible architecture, excessive props, or characters blocking the route.
