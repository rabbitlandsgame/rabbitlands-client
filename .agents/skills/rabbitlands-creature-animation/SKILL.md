# Rabbit Lands creature animation

For a canine continuation, read `docs/creature-video-motion.md` and `eloria-assets/source/animations/canine/manifest.json` before selecting a fit. Seven reviewed canine profiles, prepared inputs and native curves are packaged there. Their replay command is offline; it does not infer anatomy for a new actor. Use `eloria-assets/tools/video_motion` for native fitting, surface/contact evaluation, temporal regularization and physical chain diagnostics.

Read [the motion audit](references/motion-audit.md) for the lessons behind the selected fits. Keep source evidence, fitting assumptions, skin repairs, numerical qualification and visual acceptance distinct. A generated video is a reference, not a skeleton export. A paid provider job or non-public upload needs its own scoped user authorization; do not generate another video to recover local work.

Preserve stable IDs, textures, binds and unrelated accepted actions. Requested skin/rest defects become explicit versioned prepared inputs. Before runtime promotion compare against the current Rabbit Lands assets, whose unrelated clips can be newer than the pilot baseline. Preserve their world-space transforms through rig repairs and neutralize new controls. `video_motion.promotion` requires an unchanged original node prefix and rest-world correspondence; it is not cross-anatomy retargeting.

Use lossless packing before considering approximate optimization. `video_motion.packing` removes unreachable storage and exactly constant interior keys while retaining clip duration. Verify all static arrays and fitted channel values, including both influence sets. Unknown binary extensions require explicit support. Geometry-only duplicate pruning can erase valid animation changes.

Persist import fidelity settings and measured walk speed at the actual model scale. Opted-in canine maps account for runtime small-creature and server scaling. Godot's glTF bake cadence is separate from optimizer/compression settings: declare the required cadence in asset metadata, track the matching importer FPS and honor it in both raw scene/library paths. Compare engine world poses to independent GLB witnesses off the bake grid; two engine imports can agree while both lose fidelity. Test loops, held endpoints, action switching, RESET and actual eight-influence meshes. Validate exported keys and between-key poses, portable replay and full-cadence multi-angle review. Keep reports bound to the exact final combined file.

Public changes belong only in the current Rabbit Lands repositories. Keep private authoring paths, provider receipts, credentials and migration records outside Git. Follow root repository instructions for branch policy and repository-scoped identity. Update the manifest and evidence whenever a dependency or selected output changes.

For the authored Rabbit/Jackalope pilot, use [rabbit-family motion](../rabbitlands-video-motion-fitting/references/leporid-transfer.md); its morph-aware native adapter and explicit skin preparations are separate from the canine replay package.

For authored cat-family pilots and native anatomical side/hinge fitting, read [native feline motion](references/native-feline-motion.md). The seven cats are user accepted; their installed behavior and public checks are documented in `docs/felid-motion.md`. Consult the current selection and publication receipts before continuing.

For grounded boar/tapir pilots, head/tusk strikes and collapse-frame singularities, read [native boar and tapir motion](references/native-boar-tapir-motion.md). The five reviewed sets and portable checks are documented in `docs/boar-motion.md`. Native anatomy, wrapper behavior and visual acceptance remain actor-specific.

For rams, ibex, sheep, bison, yak, deer and elk, read [native hoofed motion](references/native-hoofed-motion.md). It covers anatomy-based forward frames, mirrored hoof lanes, native sole tilt, horn-supported collapse and actor-specific rig/coat repairs. The seven installed ram/ibex/sheep/bison/yak sets and portable checks are documented in `docs/caprine-motion.md`. The six approved deer/elk sets and portable checks are documented in `docs/cervid-motion.md`; consult the private cervid continuation memory for the selected and published state.

For pony/horse rigs and tack, read [native equid motion](references/native-equid-motion.md). It covers whole-hoof ownership, saddle/bridle attachments, hanging-tail clearance, repaired-surface import witnesses and mount handoff. The approved Moor Pony and six reviewed family transfers, including Gallop, are documented in `docs/equid-motion.md`.

For badger, otter, wolverine and stoat transfers, read [native mustelid motion](references/native-mustelid-motion.md). It covers whole-heel ownership, heavy influence consolidation, physical tail guides, clothing attachments and continuous paw-release constraints. The seven approved installed sets and portable checks are documented in `docs/mustelid-motion.md`.

For Squirrel, Dormouse and Beaver, read [native rodent motion](references/native-rodent-motion.md). It covers physical shoulder/forearm fitting, complete facial ownership, local skin-strain checks, small-creature cadence and separate rump/hock/paddle fields. The three approved installed sets and portable checks are documented in `docs/rodent-motion.md`.

For Hedgehog, Shrew and Giant Mole, read [native insectivore motion](references/native-insectivore-motion.md). It covers quill-heavy silhouettes, short-leg guide fitting, asymmetric digging arms, complete palm/finger and heel ownership, native contact surfaces and supported collapse. Consult private continuation memory for the selected review and acceptance state.

For Axolotl and Salamander pilots, read [native amphibian motion](references/native-amphibian-motion.md). It covers outward elbow planes, gill/fin ownership and full-palm contact frames; lowest-toe contact alone cannot certify a flat planted paw.

For Gecko, Chameleon, Frilled Lizard and Iguana transfers, read [native lizard motion](references/native-lizard-motion.md). It covers physical palm frames without finger bones, continuous frill/crest/throat attachments and preserving an accepted category pilot through native anatomical fitting.

For native humanoid and goblinoid fitting, read [native humanoid motion](references/native-humanoid-motion.md). The four approved goblinoid sets and portable checks are documented in `docs/goblinoid-motion.md`.

The six approved Ogre/Troll/Giant sets, including target-specific clothing and released-wrist repairs, are documented in `docs/brute-humanoid-motion.md`. Their fitting guidance is in [native humanoid motion](references/native-humanoid-motion.md).

For the approved Highland Orc and Coal Kobold motions, including native grip, descending scratch and relaxed spare-arm framing, see `docs/orc-kobold-motion.md` and [native humanoid motion](references/native-humanoid-motion.md).

For Crystal Cave and Nightweave Spider fitting, read [native spider motion](references/native-spider-motion.md). It covers sharp-tip support, rigid armored sections, alternating tripods, target-specific leg curls and runtime attachment mapping.
