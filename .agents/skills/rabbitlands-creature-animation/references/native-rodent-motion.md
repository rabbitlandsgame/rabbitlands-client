# Native rodent motion

Use for Squirrel, Dormouse and Beaver, and consult these lessons when fitting other small ground mammals. The approved installed sets and portable checks are documented in `docs/rodent-motion.md`. Numerical checks, human acceptance and publication are separate states; consult the current selection before continuing an authoring stage.

## Anatomical guides and complete surfaces

Names and source weights are insufficient anatomy. Squirrel shoulder guides were above the visible sockets; Dormouse guides were too low. Measure physical shoulder, elbow and paw sections from several views, then prepare guides and inverse binds explicitly while preserving the intended rest surface. Rebase inherited local channels and verify their imported behavior. Do not transfer one actor's absolute coordinates to another.

Find complete physical forearm and paw sections on welded topology. A dominant-weight rule can inherit wrong-leg ownership, while a spatial center strip can split the inner arm. A connected low-body region can also join both forelegs through the sternum: separate anatomical regions before collecting seeds, and collect both sides against the original weights before repainting either side. Retain rigid contact soles, coherent arm cores and broad continuous socket transitions. Avoid anchoring rigid belly skin immediately against an articulated elbow.

Protect the complete skull, eyes, muzzle, ears and lower cheek from limb leakage. A torso/socket mask can overwrite the back of an ear or lower muzzle. Fit the chin boundary to native anatomy and smooth the remaining neck band on welded topology between coherent head, torso and limb cores. Check final ownership after all repair passes.

A posterior tail mask can capture heels and upper rump fur; a low-height heel mask can capture front feet without a fore/hind bound. Beaver needs separate rump, hock, stem and broad paddle fields. Keep the paddle coherent from its stem rather than applying a bushy six-control wave. Consolidate only controls intentionally sharing the authored transform, retain useful influences and declare any skin preparation. Do not silently rank-truncate significant weights to fit eight slots.

## Timing, support and evidence

Uniform gait retiming preserves stride and contact phase only when every sampler input is shortened consistently. Update cycle metadata, measured stride speed at the existing registry scale and scrolling review ground together. Render fast loops with enough frames at playback rate one. The accepted rodent cycles are 0.70 s Walk and 0.36 s Jog/scurry; these are selections for these actors, not universal small-creature durations.

After any skin repair, reevaluate the full collapse clearance and support envelope. A corrected rest surface can put the new skin below the floor during the old motion. Constrain native head and paddle angles when solving support; a mathematically supported pose can still twist the head excessively. Check the resulting surface and foot release continuously before preserving an old floor-lift curve.

A zero coincident-vertex gap does not establish good deformation. Local edge strain exposed severe eye/forearm stretching that global volume checks missed. Keep fixed anatomical abdomen and rigid facial patches, local defect-edge measurements, complete paw tracks and independent imported-surface probes. A volume patch spanning shoulder sockets measures articulated chest tissue, so choose the abdomen region before interpreting the result. Report residual local strain separately from seam continuity and human acceptance.

## Promotion

Pin the accepted files and compare the current source GLB and registry before installing. Preserve topology, UVs, embedded images, node hierarchy, actor scale/facing and attachment metadata; declare intentional guide, rest/bind, tail and skin changes separately. Include repaired surfaces in all seven slot witnesses and eight-influence client checks. Keep dedicated maps, actor-specific importer identity, required bake cadence and lossless settings with the installed models.

The portable rodent test checks all seven slot witnesses, gait/action contact, serial lengths, terminal holds, fixed abdomen/face patches and Beaver paddle rigidity. Keep witnesses and preservation reports bound to the final combined file. High-resolution sources, rejected drafts and review videos remain ignored; only the selected runtime models and compact verification material belong in Git.
