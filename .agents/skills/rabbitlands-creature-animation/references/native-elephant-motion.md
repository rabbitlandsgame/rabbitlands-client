# Native elephant motion

Use this reference for Elephant drafts with short source leg helpers, a curved trunk, broad feet, tusks and large pinnae. Reuse motion intent only after assessing the actual actor. A successful numerical draft still requires six-angle visual inspection and human review before promotion.

## Anatomy and ownership

Measure shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee and ankle positions against actual triangle cross-sections. Named upper-leg helpers can sit near the lower limb and omit the visible upper section. Add physical sockets and intermediate hinges when evidence requires them; compensate original parenting and prove original world rest, bind prefix and the declared prepared rest surface. Newly weighted guide binds must retain the common prepared rest skin transform.

Trace the full curved trunk, including a backward-curled tip, rather than taking only the forward silhouette. Separate skull, ivory, pinna, trunk and tail ownership. An inherited trunk label can own much of the head, and stray limb labels can occur in the ear roots. Rigid tusks and a detached tail tuft need complete coherent ownership; pinna roots can blend while their outer cores remain coherent.

Texture samples at UV vertices can land in atlas padding and falsely classify trunk vertices as ivory. Combine anatomical bounds with several interior barycentric face samples. Weld the decision at exact source positions and make complete weights equal for coincident duplicates. Check their animated separation explicitly: ordinary mesh-edge strain can miss a hole between disconnected UV copies. Fair only diagnosed soft transitions through welded adjacency, with complete plantar rows, rigid body cores and appendages anchored. Restrict candidate influences to the actual anatomical neighborhood.

## Grounded locomotion

Use measured ankle/wrist alignment and the sole's native offset when choosing neutral foot targets. Forcing sole centroids directly beneath sockets can demand a deep crouch even when the visible limb should be tall. Retain paired touchdown origins and measure successive alternating steps in a moving-ground frame.

A purposeful brisk four-beat walk suits this large actor; preserve supporting contacts rather than adding flight simply because the runtime slot is named Jog. Keep bulk quiet and let the upper limbs and knees carry the stride. Include broad round foot pads, assess complete plantar coverage and tilt, and keep the whole paw above the floor.

Near maximum extension, a tiny unreachable target can produce a rapid knee snap when inverse kinematics hits its reach clamp. A small supported body-height correction can retain stride, timing and exact planted targets while restoring smooth angular motion. Audit every exported key and midpoint; playback-cadence sampling alone can miss the snap. For a single-action repair, preserve all other action arrays exactly and recheck full-surface clearance and contact.

## Attack and collapse

Use rigid tusk tips as attack probes for a forward supported shove; the flexible trunk is a separate appendage. Curl it clear of the impact path. Keep ivory shape and native trunk-section lengths independently checked.

For collapse, a low trunk can force whole-body clearance upward and prevent visible leg folding. A coherent early protective curl allows the head and knees to drop before the body rolls onto its flank. Choose releases per physical leg: the lower-side paw may support longer, while the upper-side paw must release before its chain exceeds reach. Audit actual sole motion against those per-leg times, including between-key poses.

Fold the lower pinna about the measured head-frame axis. Keep released local section offsets, match incoming angular velocity at release and use elapsed-time clearance filtering. Full-surface floor checks include trunk, ivory, ears, paws and tuft even when those appendages are excluded from head/chest/pelvis load-support definitions.

Rounded skull and torso regions need explicit physical support criteria. Distinguish a narrow coplanar optimizer target from the final supported-flank acceptance criterion; retain the existing final floor/contact limits and disclose residual regional clearance. A failed optimizer target is not proof of exact coplanar support.
