## Strike and collapse

A broad overhand strike needs an explicit planted hold and release before the hand moves. Fit the whole arm route outside and ahead of the face, then carry the hand orientation with the forearm through the stroke and recovery. A fixed world hand orientation can create an unnatural wrist even when the centroid path is smooth. Check quaternion branches and world angular speed, and retain the non-striking knuckle and both feet as actual support where that is the choreography.

For a hunched ape, fitting a single global body pitch can leave chest and pelvis support incompatible.
