# Native boar and tapir motion

Use this reference for grounded, heavy quadrupeds with native hooves and a head-led strike. These are authored motion studies; a reference video was not used for this family. The current private selection, candidates and review state live in the suid continuation memory under the ignored authoring workspace. All five reviewed sets have user visual acceptance and approval for integration. Installed behavior and portable checks are documented in `docs/boar-motion.md`; consult current publication receipts before continuing.

## Transfer behavior through anatomy

Share timing and support intent, then fit each native rig. A four-beat walk, diagonal trot without flight, rear-hoof brace, forehoof strike step and staggered recovery suit the approved base. Retain forward body support until the reaching front hooves start returning. In this family, at least two hooves support the full attack; a larger step and early torso recovery can overreach an otherwise valid chain.

Check the full head and tusk surface during anticipation. Long downward snouts can cross the ground under a wind-up angle that works on a shorter snout. Adapt the wind-up and crouch while preserving the stronger upward sweep. Measure physical muzzle travel in world space; angle signs alone do not prove forward reach. The short-legged tapir needed a deeper crouch and shorter forestep for a head-led shove.

Select the three IK roles from actual positions, hierarchy and visible anatomy. An ornate hind leg may include a short subdivision near its knee: use an appropriate chord and retain every serial segment and attachment within its native segment group. A named distal control may have no existing skin ownership. Find the physical low hoof through its whole leg ancestry, then feather a scoped repair into the native distal master. Preserve geometry, rest, binds, textures and inherited curves in a versioned prepared input.

A locked sole does not certify the rest of a limb. Check the full skin against the floor; in the Berry Boar, slightly less body drop prevented a proximal rear-leg surface from passing through the ground. Inspect original topology strain and coincident seams at the same poses, separately from the contact pass.

## Collapse articulation

A signed flexion plane is useful only while the limb chord remains sufficiently transverse to its normal. In the tapir collapse, the interpolated free-leg direction crossed that normal, creating a brief large knee swivel despite valid lengths, locked support feet and smooth body motion. More keys and a complete per-segment frame did not fix that singularity.

For that mode, transport the native bend frame from the body's rotated rest chord to the current limb chord, then construct full segment frames from the transported bend. Check that the transport's reference and target chords do not themselves become antipodal. Keep this adaptation scoped to the affected rig and action; it does not require changing qualified boar gait or collapse planes. Fit the free-paw tuck and the full skinned floor envelope together: repeated floor projection can amplify a poor tuck. The tapir used a narrower inward tuck and a newly fitted body/head/tail settling pose.

Measure local and world rotations through the transition, including between exported keys. Endpoint images and a coarse floor/reach pass missed the original brief tapir swivel. Review side, front, game, rear and opposite views at the same seconds as well as normal-speed playback.

## Native import and evidence

Test inherited actions through the actual client importer. Three ornate boars all contain wrapper translation tracks, but only Brambleback's nonconstant wrapper caused material idle/hit witness errors. Bake that motion into the physical root bones while neutralizing the wrapper track. Preserve primary-bone worlds and full skinned surfaces at original breakpoints and between them; report this as a world-preserving adaptation, not unchanged animation-channel bytes.

Bound float32 contact compensation and translation interpolation refinement to measured numerical residuals, preserving native lengths, angles, events and stride. Recheck supported-surface velocity between keys and the current client's bake cadence. This family's existing dense source and import settings are measured local choices, not a universal rate prescription.

Bind validation, fresh imports, review media and selected files to actual hashes. If reusing numerical evidence for unchanged clips, prove exact sampler values and static skin/geometry/bind/rest/texture arrays. Recheck changed inherited world motion separately. Keep source preparation, numerical qualification, engine playback and user visual acceptance distinct.

## Hanging foliage and skin-only repairs

Inspect whole physical foliage pieces when brambles stretch behind a limb. In Brambleback, two hanging pieces mixed tail-root/chain weights with both thighs, knees and ankles. The visible tear came from independent joint pulls across the same piece. A coherent attachment to each piece's actual root removed the stretch while retaining the root's motion; this is not a prescription to bind every ornament to the body. Choose the attachment from native geometry and anatomy.

For component analysis, account for duplicated UV and split-normal vertices. Raw triangle connectivity fragmented these two brambles into small face groups. Welding equal source positions for analysis recovered complete pieces; repair all corresponding duplicates together, while leaving the exported geometry, UVs, normals and topology intact. Compare unchanged weight rows exactly and version the prepared skin. Do not change motion to hide an attachment defect.

Quantify the requested piece at identical old/new poses and original edges, and inspect it from both rear quarters at normal speed. A whole-model percentile can hide a severely stretched small ornament. Independent bone witnesses cannot establish correct skinning: include affected skinned-surface positions in fresh-import checks. Exclude importer-created custom bone-shape meshes when identifying the actual deforming skin. Recheck the full surface floor and support patches after a weight-only change, even when animation samplers and sole weights are exact.
