# Pectoral-fin crawler drafts

Use this approach only after inspecting an actor with comparable native anatomy, such as Mudskipper. It shares a qualification framework with aquatic and legless actors, not their body poses or contact roles.

Inventory connected components before assigning anatomical ownership. Mudskipper has separate pectoral fans, two eye globes and a dorsal panel; its main body and caudal fan form a curved mesh. Source bone positions substantially missed the visible tail and asymmetric fan roots. Measure actual main-skin plane sections and each fan's proximal surface and contact edge. The two fan pivots have signed winding 1. The open/overlapping main head has fractional winding, so certify local section enclosure with actual positive surface intersections in both X and Y directions rather than pretending it is a closed solid.

Append measured body controls and explicit fan, skull and dorsal ownership in a versioned prepared input. Preserve static geometry, original node-world-rest and inverse-bind prefixes, appearance and runtime files. Use local longitudinal weights for the curved trunk and a smooth transition into a complete rigid caudal fan. Coherent eyes and complete pectoral cores must not inherit unrelated body motion. Check detached panel roots visually: rigid component shape alone cannot certify attachment continuity.

An alternating fin-crutch crawl and a quicker paired-fin skip need different support phases. Fit each source fan's actual edge and pivot independently. Keep measured body-segment lengths while correcting the body's horizontal alignment. Use a low forward body bump with a pectoral brace, then a supported forward settlement with separate pelvis, belly, chest, chin, fin and tail regions.

Match recovery velocity to the contact phase at both ends of the cycle. A smoothstep return with zero endpoint speed creates a pause when the planted phase has nonzero speed. A quintic return with matching endpoint slope avoids that stop; use a lift envelope whose first and second derivatives vanish at release and landing.

Measure exported fin-edge advance on both sides under one common travel speed, contact height, edge tilt and forward residual. Report the small lateral contact arc separately: near-zero forward residual is not proof of zero lateral sliding. Keep dorsal, eye, skull and fan shape checks separate from full-surface strain, joint turns, held-ending support, independent fresh Blender/current-Godot qualification and actual six-angle inspection. Keep new sets unreviewed local drafts until deliberate promotion.

The first Mudskipper six-angle review exposed a dorsal-base gap during the grounded ending even though the complete rigid panel passed shape tests. Actual point-to-triangle distances to the main body showed about 0.079H extra separation. Driving the lower dorsal rows with the same local trunk fields and fading into an upper rigid core reduced death separation below0.0017H; all-action increase stayed below0.0028H. Preserve the original body-to-root distances, audit the exported surface, and rerun replay, imports and review after this explicit skin change. A complete rigid detached panel is appropriate only when its attachment remains coherent through every action.
