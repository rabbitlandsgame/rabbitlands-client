# Native Penguin and Pelican motion

Treat these as separate families with actor-specific movement and contact rules. Consult their private continuation record for selected source, qualification and review state. Penguin has one mesh section, zero original corrective targets and seven ground actions. Pelican has nine sections, seventeen original targets per section and a native Fly clip. Preserve the complete source prefix and every material section; do not invent a Penguin flight action.

## Webbing and the actual ankle

Select the complete floor-connected webbing through welded topology, including medial toes and dark claws. Calibrate the neutral target to the selected foot surface's actual centroid. A misplaced neutral path can already stretch an ankle while standing. Fit the ankle hinge inside the visible joint; the generic foot or knee guide is not that hinge. Penguin's short tarsus particularly needed a forward ankle pivot and a separate populated shin band. Keep whole feet and shin probes rigid while allowing the actual intervening ankle skin to flex. Derive the middle-shin audit band from actual single-control ownership and keep it above the blended ankle rows. A nominal height band that includes ankle blend vertices measures joint flex as shin-size change. Assert the probe ownership explicitly and retain the same physical shape thresholds when correcting its scope. Match gait reach to this anatomy, then compare successive world advances under one ground speed.

Consolidate Penguin flipper-tip ownership through its proximal wing control for ground actions. Mixed source weights at nearly coincident tip rows can tear during a small flare. Keep the shoulder transition smooth and check it from front and rear.

## Bill, body support and release

Fit the complete hard bill to the skull while treating the Pelican's low rear pouch as a flexible neck transition. A single forward-coordinate cutoff can either tear the bill or drag the whole pouch through the ground. Inspect the throat and rear head as well as the tip. Bill rigidity probes should describe the hard bill, not silently include the flexible pouch.

Measure support on the real ventral sternum. Pelican's high forward chest is not its lowest supporting breast surface; use source anatomy and opposing views to identify the actual sternum and belly. Keep bill contact separate from body support. Released feet may need to move in height as the body folds: continuous three-dimensional reach constraints preserve both physical and generic chains where a horizontal-only projection fails. Recheck the floor, shin shape and complete release path.

Penguin's long source tail needs to settle about a centered proximal attachment when the upright body falls forward. Apply a tail-floor limit to the moving tail surface, with a small numerical tolerance; including unchanged planted feet in that test can switch the tail adjustment on and off because of roundoff. Inspect the full attachment and UV seams.

## Complete plans and import fidelity

A cached surface plan must sample the complete actor adapter. Calling only an ancestor's surface method silently omitted the new tail correction even though direct pose evaluation included it. Bind cached plans to the source, calibration and all fitting dependencies, and verify the selected export contains the new surface motion.

Pelican's textureless repair sections use vertex colours. Check their albedo flags in both cached and raw game imports. Inspect the actual final actor appearance call path as well: a tint helper only repairs meshes whose names reach that branch, and a surface-finish helper does not automatically enable vertex colours. Preserve bare-import failures in the receipt. A local draft may use explicit per-instance material preparation that duplicates only affected materials and changes only the colour-albedo flag; this does not qualify the unmodified runtime appearance. Record this as a required integration correction and keep the source model untouched. A preview-only padded colour bake must keep original GLB data unchanged and isolate shared preview materials where separate meshes need different charts. Bind independent geometry witnesses before changing preview UVs. Frame native flight from its own complete animated extents instead of inheriting another bird's camera.

Use the native, physical, interpolation and fresh-import qualification described in [waterfowl motion](native-anatid-motion.md). Numerical checks, browser playback and human acceptance remain separate; large authoring exports require measured optimization before runtime promotion.
