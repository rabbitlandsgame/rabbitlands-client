# Native Kangaroo biped motion

Use an explicit biped adapter for a kangaroo: paired hind-foot hopping, small free arms, large haunches and a long balancing tail. The Rabbit/Jackalope category is useful contact mathematics, but its four-limb choreography and native skin fields do not establish Kangaroo anatomy. Keep new drafts separate from artistic approval and runtime installation.

## Anatomy and skin

Verify pivots against actual closed triangles and horizontal surface sections. A contained hip/knee/ankle proposal can still be almost straight; measure neutral perpendicular knee offset, signed bend and extension margin before posing. Recover the anterior hind-knee bend and posterior elbow bend in the actor's forward frame rather than inheriting a quadruped hinge normal.

Inspect full physical skin fields as well as joint locations. On the current Kangaroo, native hind-leg influences also reached hanging fingertips, while some visible shin tissue followed the pelvis. Use measured spatial separation and source influences together. A forward-Z arm mask needs a lower height boundary: long hind toes also extend forward and must not enter the hand field. Reassess full upper/hinge/distal tissue, welded seams and complete soles after each repair; a correct rigid sole does not prove the surrounding leg is attached correctly.

A skull field based only on height can leave the forward snout attached to the neck. Include the actual muzzle, both eyes and detached facial islands, with a controlled throat transition. A central mouth island alone does not establish an articulating jaw.

Preserve original node IDs, world rest, bind prefix, geometry, normals, UVs, textures and materials through compensated guide parenting. Bind new weighted guides using the measured common rest-skin matrix. Weld exact-position skin duplicates before and after fairing. If fairing spreads small tail weights across too many guides, consolidate the tail total and mean arc coordinate onto adjacent controls explicitly, then requalify deformation. Do not silently truncate significant influences or write floating-point JOINTS accessors.

## Paired hopping and attack

Measure both complete sole centroids in the same moving-ground frame. The hind feet touch down together, so an alternating left/right step-event ratio is the wrong gait audit. Check equal paired forward travel, actual repeated touchdown advances, negative stance speed, airborne lift and endpoint velocity continuity. Check reach throughout flight: late airborne body height can exhaust extension even when the contact phase is valid. Repair the body arc or target path without stretching bones.

Keep small hands ahead of and clear of the haunches. A guarded single hind-foot kick can retain the opposite planted foot and coherent tail counterbalance; this is a chosen authored action, not an inferred reference-video measurement. Do not claim that the tail carries body load merely because it approaches the floor.

The low tail can penetrate during a small crouch or backward recoil. Solve coherent rooted tail pitch against the evaluated tail surface while retaining serial lengths and a centered horizontal lane. Avoid an isolated tip correction or whole-actor lift that breaks planted feet. Recheck exported keys, midpoints and angular continuity after the clearance solve.

## Supported forward collapse

Fit disjoint physical pelvic, thoracic and skull support patches against the complete deformed surface. Include actual ventral torso tissue and exclude misplaced limb ownership. Head-only or incomplete skull patches can certify a pose while the actual chin reaches the floor first. A balanced endpoint orientation also needs an explicit root descent; clearance-only upward projection can leave the torso hovering.

Keep the opening feet planted, release them explicitly, then transport length-preserving limb rotations into relaxed poses. Settle the neck/skull, hands, heels and tail with the body, and hold the terminal pose exactly. Inspect the planted opening and terminal body from multiple angles. Preserve failed trials and their measurements rather than increasing the contact tolerance.

## Qualification

Bind reports to the exact combined candidate. Require source/static preservation and guarded raw rig/skin replay; full-surface strain; exported-key and midpoint floor, contact, serial length, hinge-lane and turn checks; fresh Blender surface/bone probes; and the current Godot importer/resolver with independent off-grid surfaces and normals. Produce all actions at native speed in six views and inspect the actual pose sequences. Dense export and import cadences are measured actor-specific choices. Technical qualification, local footage, browser playback, human acceptance and installation are distinct states.
