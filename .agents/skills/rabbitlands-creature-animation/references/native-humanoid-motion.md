# Native humanoid fitting

For goblinoids and other biped pilots, audit the current imported roster, native rig, hand equipment and animation map before choosing a donor. Hobgoblin, Mire Goblin, Gremlin and Moss Bugbear are user accepted native fits; their installed action maps and portable checks are documented in `docs/goblinoid-motion.md`. Consult private continuation memory for current review and installation state.

## Shared-library calibration

The shared Universal Animation Library contains a `Rest_Pose` clip. Its node defaults are a posed state, not the anatomical reference. Evaluate `Rest_Pose` at zero. Calibrate each native upper/lower limb direction against that reference, then apply donor world-space direction and roll deltas. A donor T pose and target A pose cannot be reconciled by copying local quaternions or applying an uncalibrated world rotation delta. Strip donor matrix scale before extracting rotations.

Use donor motion as intent. Preserve native serial lengths and actor proportions, and solve the two feet against actual sole/heel surfaces. Fit the pelvis to reachable target contacts rather than stretching a leg to match donor proportions. Derive movement speed from target stride, clip duration and the unchanged registry scale. Confirm the actor's equipment: Hobgoblin has no weapon or finger controls, so the attack slot uses an unarmed forward strike with its existing sculpted hand shape.

## Support and preservation

Measure complete native feet, including heels, and retain each sole's neutral tilt. Lock stance translation and rotation; release continuously into swing. During collapse, floor correction after release must participate in convergence: a centered contact probe is insufficient if the toe or heel still penetrates. Qualify head, torso and released limbs against the ground, plus the terminal hold. Keep visual acceptance separate from numeric support checks.

Make coherent face, hair, hand, muscle and clothing ownership explicit in a versioned prepared skin. Preserve geometry, UVs, normals, images, hierarchy and binds. Check seams and local strain through the complete motion; a rigid torso core alone cannot qualify joint transitions or clothing.

Select export/import cadence from measured between-key error. The first Hobgoblin fit passed sampled key poses at 384 Hz but failed dense support-velocity checks between keys. The selected pilot uses 3072 Hz with matching importer metadata/policy. Gremlin's transferred run needs 6144 Hz to pass the same between-key support-speed limit; refine only the failing clip while preserving unrelated curves, and ensure both tracked import policy and the runtime fidelity helper honor the declaration. This is measured per fit, not a fixed minimum for all humanoids.

## Fresh-import review

Retained clips can animate corrective morphs. In Blender's slotted actions, activate both armature `OBJECT` and shape-key `KEY` slots, mute competing NLA tracks and reset unused shape values when switching. The first Hobgoblin preview matched bones while its retained hit reaction missed a corrective shape and differed by about 0.004 native units. Activating the morph slot restored skin agreement below 0.000001. Do not change a retained game clip to compensate for a preview adapter omission.

Bind independent off-grid bone and skinned-surface witnesses to the exact final file. Verify all seven runtime slots, eight influences where present, action switching, RESET, idle restoration, loops and held endpoints. Blender can exit successfully after a Python assertion; require successful witness receipts, an explicit render completion marker and exact frame counts. Review side, front, rear and both quarter views at normal speed before family transfer.

## Thigh, calf and waist corrections

Fixed skeleton lengths and a rigid torso do not certify the limb skin. The first Hobgoblin walk retained substantial thigh-bone swing while thigh vertices followed the pelvis; calf vertices mixed pelvis, shin and foot motion. The right calf's anatomically selected muscle core lost up to 37% volume during the punch. Select thigh/calf probes from the physical surface and topology, independently of existing influence mass. Measure their volume, principal lengths and agreement with the intended segment transform through the whole clip. Keep these core checks distinct from soft knee/ankle transitions and full-surface strain.

Audit physical joint placement as well as ownership. The generic Hobgoblin knees were anterior to the skin envelope. Its explicit prepared-rig repair moves the leg pivots inside the measured hip/knee/ankle chains and updates the inverse binds, preserving the rest surface, geometry and original node identities. Rebase retained animation translations so each bone's deformation matrix, `world_pose * inverse(rest_world)`, remains unchanged; preserve rotation, scale and corrective morph samplers. Comparing raw bone positions would incorrectly reject the declared pivot change, while comparing only rest geometry would miss altered inherited motion. Bind the repair receipt and independent engine/Blender witnesses to the final combined model.

A separate belt or buckle can appear detached even with zero welded seam gaps. Identify attachment islands from topology; fit a continuous lower-torso/pelvis field across the body and skirt, and give rigid fasteners one coherent owner. Compare physical belt-to-body anchor separation over time, rather than certifying every accessory by torso-core volume. Preserve earlier review versions and, when useful, provide synchronized before/after playback so changes in visible thigh motion and calf shape can be reviewed directly.

## Complete arm surfaces and joint transitions

A rigid muscle-core check can pass while elbow, shoulder or knee transition faces still fold or spike. Hobgoblin's second review exposed arm collapse during run/death and a hard calf break after the core-volume repair. Evaluate the complete joint regions, including low-area triangles, edge stretch, pose-dependent crease angles and every viewing side. Keep the unchanged low-poly rest facets distinct from added deformation creases.

Do not cut limb ownership with a single lateral coordinate threshold. Hobgoblin's inner forearm crossed that threshold and produced isolated spikes during the punch even after the outer arm looked repaired. Use connected-surface distances from anatomical arm and torso seeds, with a continuous shoulder transition, so an entire forearm participates coherently. The vest and armhole edges need their own torso attachment field; do not infer their owner from nearby moving skin.

Intermediate bend controls can preserve volume through broad transitions. The selected refinement adds shoulder, elbow and knee blend controls between neighboring segment deformation rotations, at the common physical joint pivot. Emit their actual local tracks in every authored and retained clip, and include them in off-grid witnesses, RESET and action-switch tests. Check temporal continuity of the rotation interpolation; append controls without changing original node identities.

When a few large faces span a bend, local conforming edge subdivision can let the deformation curve without altering the rest silhouette. Preserve the original vertex prefix; interpolate UVs, normals and every corrective target at inserted edge points, weld skin ownership across UV splits, and propagate edge splits into neighboring faces. Verify per-source-triangle oriented coverage and the unchanged rest surface, images and inherited deformation matrices. A topology refinement is an explicit prepared-input change, not an unchanged-mesh claim.

For before/after surface checks after refinement, reconstruct old posed edge midpoints barycentrically so both measurements represent the same original surface. Keep original anatomical core probes unchanged and add separate transition-region measurements; do not silently shrink a failing test region. The third Hobgoblin pilot retains its calf/thigh and forearm/upper-arm cores while reducing the upper-tail distribution of added elbow and knee creases in all four motions. Visual approval remains separate from these numerical checks.

## Crouched goblinoid family transfer

Bone names alone do not identify anatomy. Measure the visible knee, hock, ankle, heel and toe surfaces before selecting a chain. Mire Goblin fits a physical hip/knee/foot chain while retaining its intermediate guide. Moss Bugbear needs its native knee/hock/ankle arrangement: fit the upper two segments to a compound hock/ankle/paw effector, preserving the ankle offset. Collapsing that arrangement into a human ankle leaves its posterior heel fur in a shin frame and can drive it through the floor. Separate calf, hock and paw ownership along the complete native anatomical arc; a vertical height field cannot distinguish neighboring segments in a deeply crouched leg. Keep the accepted pilot's exact model and media when building a family comparison.

Native controls can describe attachments rather than the apparent anatomical role. Mire Goblin's multiple `tail_*` chains drive loincloth strips. Moss Bugbear's `snout` parent also influences the hanging beard, while its actual head is a child. Inspect weighted surfaces before consolidating a head subtree or including a control in torso-support measurements. Fit hanging clothing separately during collapse so it can settle without lifting the torso to clear a rigid flap. A genuine long tail needs a coherent root attachment and gradual bend toward the tip; cancelling the pelvis rotation at the first tail segment produces a sharp rump kink.

Average influence fields across welded UV duplicates before constructing physical ownership regions. Use dominant limb membership to exclude low cross-side leakage, and preserve already prepared fields when processing the next limb. Otherwise a second limb pass can quietly restore old weights in the first limb's muscle core. Keep the physical core probes and inspect their actual final influence fields before spending time on a dense export; a source-mass threshold alone does not guarantee final ownership.

For short legs, fit a smooth reachable pelvis envelope with a finite knee-bend margin. A nearly straight knee amplifies interpolation error and can flip when a donor-derived pole crosses the hip-to-ankle line. Stabilize the anatomical anterior knee plane during support and release it continuously toward the prone plane. Check the exported animation between keys rather than accepting the author's analytic foot lock. Once both feet release during collapse, residual whole-body floor correction can move the entire rig; applying it during planted support would break contact. Preserve serial lengths and verify the final result independently.

Frame cameras from the complete motion bounds, including a collapsed body's forward displacement and a long tail. Rest bounds can hide the head or tail even when the file itself is intact. Qualify native sole arches and toe/heel underside thickness separately from the level contact frame; do not claim every irregular toe surface lies in a single geometric plane.

A smooth donor curve can still acquire a wrist flip when a target blend chooses the shortest relative rotation independently at every frame. Mire Goblin's right wrist crossed the half-turn boundary during collapse and jumped despite otherwise stable limb lengths and soles. Choose a continuous quaternion branch relative to the start of the release, then interpolate its unwrapped axis angle. Increasing cadence alone cannot repair a branch discontinuity; locate the offending control and time before resampling. The same branch check applies to nested released-foot rotation blends: Mire Goblin crossed the half-turn twice. Interpolating once between fixed neutral and prone foot orientations removed those turns while retaining planted contact and the other actions.

Reset the imported armature object's transform to the glTF static defaults as well as resetting pose bones and shape keys. Blender can retain a small object translation from an authored clip when its action is cleared. That offset contaminated rest-surface matching on Moss Bugbear, even though activated clip witnesses matched closely. Reset object defaults before rest binding and every clip switch; retained clips may have no wrapper-object tracks. Keep the strict rest and off-grid surface checks rather than widening their tolerance.

The native runtime library maps bone tracks, so outer armature-object translation can be absent even when two engine import paths agree locally. Bake translation-only wrapper motion into the direct skeleton-root translation before promotion, including retained source idles/hit reactions. Keep the wrapper rest transform and original hierarchy, use the union of both linear translation knot sets, and prove physical joint/surface worlds remain unchanged off-grid. Compare physical skin joints and rendered surfaces for preservation; the declared wrapper track itself becomes constant. Independent world-space witnesses catch this omission where local bone-pose comparisons do not.


## Target guards and released death legs

Direction-calibrated donor arms can preserve motion intent while putting large native hands above a target's head. Fit the physical hand centroid to a target guard with two-bone arm IK, preserving serial lengths and elbow clearance. Check the actual head and hand surfaces as well as shoulder pivots: Moss Bugbear's shoulder centers can sit above its visible face in the transferred strike. Keep the reach frame horizontal for the attack, and account for the native wrist-to-hand offset. Include time zero in pose sheets; the old attack sheet began after the starting guard.

A requested one-two scratch is a separate attack shape, not a relabeled punch. Author alternating outward windups and crossing, descending claw sweeps, with overlap between recovery and the second preparation. Preserve native hand geometry and keep the support constraints. Keep the attack slot and gameplay duration unless the intended action requires changing them.

Floor clearance and fixed segment lengths do not certify anatomical knees. A release pole interpolating toward positive world up can fold knees through the torso. Inspect knee lanes and the complete hip/knee/ankle path, using the visible hip-to-shoulder frame as well as the deformed pelvis axes; crouched native rigs have different rest torso directions. Blend from hanging-leg goals toward prone goals with a continuous downward/outward knee lane. Retain compound hock/ankle arrangements.

After both feet release, do not feed a per-foot floor deficit back into the free ankle goal while also applying whole-body floor correction. That feedback can push the ankle toward the hip, fold the knee and introduce rapid reversals even though bone lengths remain fixed. Use consistent released-limb goals and whole-body support after release, and keep planted contact correction before release. Recheck the full fall and terminal torso support after changing leg goals; a joint-direction check alone can accept a body that no longer collapses naturally. Review the late transition between the old pose-sheet samples, not only the endpoint.

For action-only revisions, replace only the affected clips, prove the unchanged locomotion and retained actions against the prior selected file, and preserve the accepted pilot's exact model and media. Rebind native, fresh-import and engine evidence to the final wrapper-baked file. Keep sampled anatomical checks, rendered review and user acceptance distinct.


## Longer diagonal arm strokes

For a replacement swipe, freeze the accepted starting guard relative to each arm's torso anchor, then attach it to the current torso motion. Using the previous attack's moving arm as the recovery target can contaminate the new stroke. Distinguish the shoulder pivot from the physical hand centroid: retract and elevate the native clavicle where present, or use a measured shoulder arc when no clavicle exists, and account for the native wrist-to-hand offset when placing the wind-up beside the head.

A broad stroke can reach full extension while maintaining native segment lengths. Author elbow flex explicitly through wind-up, extension, straight-arm descent and recovery, then derive reach from the two segment lengths. Deriving flex from an unconstrained hand target crossing the reach sphere can create a sudden bend during recovery even with smooth targets. Recover along a forward arc so the hand does not pass through the shoulder. Use stable anatomical hinge and hand-roll frames; an antiparallel vector alignment or independently chosen half-turn branch can twist the wrist or shoulder blend. Bind helper quaternion branches to a fixed guard reference and check angular continuity before dense export.

Measure the final exported hand path, cross-body displacement, downward travel, shoulder lift/retraction and straight-phase elbow angle in the target's anatomical frame. For a mirrored combo, check both hands and distinguish faster stroke timing from total gameplay-slot duration. Keep the previously approved death and locomotion samplers unchanged when only the attack is revised. Provide wind-up, full-extension and follow-through poses from both sides as well as the front and rear.


For broad swipes, certify the complete forearm and hand route around the head, not just the hand centroid at the wind-up. Mire Goblin needed a wider guard and elbow pole, a lower cross-body arc and an outward recovery arc; its left shoulder's higher native placement required separate clearance tuning. A fixed facial-volume proxy can locate troublesome times, while six-angle renders distinguish real intersections from conservative proxy overlap. Keep the probe vertex set fixed when comparing revisions.

A longer or lowered resting arm can amplify inherited torso angular hitches that were less visible with a compact boxing guard. Measure world-space hand acceleration and the body contribution before smoothing only the wrist. For a supported in-place attack, an explicitly authored smooth torso wind-up and follow-through can replace the old punch's body timing while preserving the planted leg pose. Attach a relaxed non-striking arm in the target's walking torso frame rather than copying its world-space hand position.

A zero welded-seam gap does not exclude tearing within a triangle. Moss Bugbear had two coincident toe vertices with the same incorrect calf ownership; their neighboring foot edges stretched almost ninefold in the run despite passing seam checks. Inspect the worst physical edge through the full motion, correct the complete UV-equivalent point, preserve weights elsewhere, and add those vertices to independent import witnesses. Verify all adjacent edges and complete-foot contact, then compare the original worst walk/run phases from several angles. A skin-only repair intentionally affects rendered surfaces in retained clips while their animation samplers stay unchanged.

To exaggerate a reach-constrained swipe, enlarge the shoulder wind-up, angular hand route and torso follow-through together. Scaling an already unreachable hand target alone cannot extend fixed native segments. Compare the actual exported hand path and vertical/lateral spans with the accepted version, while checking full extension, relaxed off-hand framing and unchanged planted-leg worlds. Keep approved neighboring actors byte-exact during an actor-only refinement.

For a metric endpoint request, convert through the declared model/game scale and actor-forward transform, then measure the deformed hand at the stated strike time. A Cartesian forward offset can exceed reach mid-swing even when the requested ending point is reachable. A calibrated angular correction on the fixed reach sphere, blended into the late strike and recovery, can preserve full extension and native segment lengths. Record secondary lateral/vertical displacement and verify that the early attack, other controls and planted feet retain their prior worlds.

A rigid-ownership-only clearance probe can omit the offending elbow surface. In the Bugbear swipe, the last intersections were mixed forearm/helper vertices even after the pure forearm and hand subset cleared a facial hull. Audit complete anatomical arm and head regions with the actual deformed surfaces, including palm, fingers and blend helpers. Keep the same vertex/triangle regions in before-and-after comparisons; use enclosure tests to locate close phases and triangle intersections to verify contact. Report the sampled temporal scope rather than claiming continuous collision proof.

For a head-crossing swipe, coordinate torso turn, clavicle sweep and anatomical upper-arm/forearm roll; the elbow bulge can still hit the head when the hand center clears. Keep the crossing route below the face and recover outward. If preserving an approved lateral and forward hand finish on a fixed reach sphere, solve the remaining downward component and recheck the whole arm. Preserving only the old projected direction can lift the elbow back into the head. Retain the off-hand in the walking torso frame and compare planted-leg worlds and unrelated clips exactly.


## Heavy humanoids, clothing and released hands

The six approved Ogre/Troll/Giant sets and portable checks are documented in `docs/brute-humanoid-motion.md`. Qualify the actual pelvis-to-chest column as well as the shoulders: forward torso rotation can leave a short-legged native actor squatting with its pelvis far behind the feet. Fit the pelvis inside the fixed-foot reach envelope, preserve native lengths and evaluate complete sole surfaces between keys. Tiny source nonuniform scales can change frame matrix components after upstream rotations; retain those scale channels and measure actual ankle/sole displacement separately.

A rigid cloth island can have edge stretch1.0 and zero welded-seam gap while visibly separating from the moving waist or thighs. Track fixed cloth-to-body surface anchors through every action. Barycentric transfer of nearby anatomical body influence fields can attach a waistband to the lower torso and lateral hems to their own thighs; preserve a hanging center in the pelvis frame. Smooth on cloth topology, keep other skin rows exact and recheck all actions. Broad coordinate-based thigh repairs can accidentally deform a separate loincloth.

Measure wrist rotation relative to the native forearm. Independent donor wrist calibration introduced near-half-turn motion on the Two-Headed Giant. For a relaxed released hand, preserve its native forearm relationship and solve the elbow/wrist route against the complete hand and forearm surfaces. Neutralizing the wrist alone can drive fingers through the ground if the old elbow remains high. Lower the release elbow plane as needed, keeping body and leg support unchanged.

Applying an ease-to-zero curve to every intermediate strike pose creates a pause at impact and follow-through. Keep progress moving through those joins and round the transitions on an already clearance-safe route. Replacing the entire position/orientation path with a broad new spline can reintroduce upper-arm/head collisions even when the club tip clears. Keep the spare arm in a stable torso/clavicle frame and audit complete arm, helper, weapon and head surfaces.

Before binary compaction, remove animation samplers unreferenced by channels and remap the channel indices. Historical fitting revisions can otherwise retain many obsolete samplers despite removing unreachable buffer views. Prove active curves, static arrays and clip duration are lossless, then bind native and fresh-import witnesses to the packed runtime hash.
