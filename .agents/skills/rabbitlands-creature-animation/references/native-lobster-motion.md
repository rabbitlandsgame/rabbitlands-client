# Native Lobster motion

This Lobster source has eight continuous outer walking limbs, eight inner pendant appendages, four antenna branches, asymmetric real forearm/palm/finger groups and a continuous segmented tail with a separate fan. It is not the separated rigid armor layout of the earlier crabs. Give continuous limb shafts narrow smooth bend fields; keep actual whole palms, fingers, forearms, antenna branches and pendant pieces rigid. Certify the rigid parts and fully owned shaft regions separately from the blended hinges.

Its generic source leg_a controls do not mark the physical pincers, and its original head guide lies ahead of the visible head. Measure actual caps and adjoining-piece sockets. For a finger hinge, use the proximal finger region so a nearly closed tip cannot be mistaken for the joint. Preserve the complete original node/rest/bind prefix and source geometry. Add measured controls and explicit review attachment names. Do not imply that a private review registry override has been installed in the runtime.

The short anterior outer legs need a more posterior stance lane while retaining the same measured world advance as all six longer limbs. A lower, narrower, slightly forward posture provides reachable source-specific steps. Check the full loop and actual surface contact, not just nominal endpoints or a common stride parameter.

Keep the already centered tail in pitch. Brace its proximal section with the carapace; use restrained correlated downstream frames, narrow ring transitions and one whole fan field. Original skin labels that mix posterior legs into the tail are not suitable ownership evidence. Measure fan centering relative to the actual body frame.

A minimum-angle carapace hull triangle can impose an unnecessary side roll on this long animal. Prefer a measured pitch-only underside support patch when actual front/rear carapace contact and the complete final limb/tail support hull pass unchanged limits. Fold the eight inner appendages before dropping the body, release continuous walking shafts without stretching, and fit the two differently oriented pincers independently. Check posterior tail and fan support separately from the front body, with a held ending and no initial global rise.

Retain source UVs, normals, topology, materials/images and unsigned skin indices. Require exact raw/full replay, meaningful exported shape/strain, length, contact, support and continuity checks, fresh independent Blender/current-Godot witnesses and actual diagnostic/final six-angle/contact inspection. When staging an asset from a subfolder such as basic_expansion, bind its import policy to the actual registry scene path. All new data and editor profiles stay in the owned task worktree; every set remains an unreviewed local draft until separately authorized.
